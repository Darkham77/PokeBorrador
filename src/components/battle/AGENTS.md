# Purpose

Manage the logic and assets of battle.

## Ownership

Frontend Developers / Systems Engineers.

## Local Contracts

- Follow standard repository modularity guidelines.
- **Declarative UI Parameterization (`BattleUiConfig`)**: `BattleArena.vue`, `BattleArenaControls.vue`, and `BattleActionButtons.vue` MUST consume reactive `uiConfig` from `battleStore` or `BattleContext` to toggle mode-specific widgets (turn clocks, spectator overlay, replay controls, bag button, forfeit button, flee button). Hardcoded mode branching or creating parallel arena components for specific modes is strictly forbidden.
- **Battle Finish Overlay Modularization (`BattleFinishOverlay.vue`)**: The post-battle victory/defeat/capture completion overlay (`.battle-finish-overlay`) is modularized into `BattleFinishOverlay.vue` to maintain `BattleArenaControls.vue` strictly under 500 lines while preserving button styling, sound triggers, and flow completion actions.
- **Emoji Typography Standard**: Every emoji rendered in battle Vue templates or overlays (such as spectator badges, replay buttons, or status labels) MUST be wrapped in a `<span class="emoji">` tag to satisfy project typographic and vertical alignment audits.
- **Move Slot Disabling during Restricted Requests & Locked States**: In `BattleMoveSlot.vue`, any move not present in `playerRequest.active[0].moves` (when Pokémon Showdown provides a restricted move subset, such as during locked moves or recharge turns) MUST evaluate `isDisabled = true` and apply `.disabled-move` styling. Additionally, when `isPokemonLocked(p)` is active, all moves differing from `p.lastMove` MUST be disabled in the UI to prevent illegal move selections.
- **Dynamic Forced Move Auto-Execution Watcher**: The forced move auto-execution watcher in `BattleArenaControls.vue` and fallback indexing in `battleTurn.ts` MUST dynamically detect all locking volatiles (`lockedmove`, `twoturnmove`, `mustrecharge`, `thrashTurns`, `uproar`, `rollout`, `bide`) rather than relying on hardcoded move IDs.
- **Auto-Battle NPC / Trainer Dialogue Delay (`AUTO_BATTLE_NPC_DIALOG_DELAY_SEC = 3.0s`)**: In auto-battle mode (`autoBattle = true`), when encountering an NPC or Trainer (`isTrainer === true` or `isGym === true`) in `SEARCH_PHASE` (`COMBAT_OR_FLEE`), the control panel in `BattleArenaControls.vue` MUST NOT auto-start the battle immediately. It MUST execute a GSAP delayed call (`gsap.delayedCall(AUTO_BATTLE_NPC_DIALOG_DELAY_SEC, ...)`) of 3.0s to ensure the dialogue speech bubble at center stage remains readable before transitioning to `FIRST_INTRO`. The delayed call MUST be killed cleanly on unmount or manual button interaction.
- **Pokéball Energy Beam Vector & TransformOrigin Geometry**:
  - When animating energy beam transitions (`catching` and `releasing`) via GSAP, `transformOrigin` MUST be specified using valid numeric percentage values aligned with the sprite's support line on the ground: `${feetXPct}% ${localGroundY.value}` (e.g. `50% 75%`). Using complex CSS `calc()` expressions inside GSAP `transformOrigin` is strictly forbidden because GSAP cannot parse arithmetic expressions and silently falls back to `bottom center` (100%), displacing the collapse anchor to the bottom of the container.
  - The target vector $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$ to the Pokéball center MUST be calculated relative to the ground origin as $\Delta x = 0$ and $\Delta y = -\text{pokeballSize} \times 0.35$, compensating for the Pokéball's CSS `translateY(-85%)` style.
  - Energy transitions MUST scale purely geometrically (`scale: 0 -> 1` or `scale: 1 -> 0`) with solid `opacity: 1`, without trailing opacity fades.
- **Lore-Proportional Item Sizing & 3D Perspective**:
  - All combat items rendered in the 3D arena (such as Poké Balls for switches, captures, and recalls) MUST derive their dimensions from `spatialCoordinator.ts` using side-specific constants (`BASE_POKEBALL_SIZE_PLAYER = 27` / 54px vs `BASE_POKEBALL_SIZE_ENEMY = 18` / 36px) multiplied by `OBJECT_SCALE`.
  - Sizing must preserve official lore proportions (e.g. 1:5 ratio with a 0.3m Pokémon like Rattata) and depth perspective between player foreground (300 units) and enemy background (200 units). Fixed CSS dimensions on combatant items in `.scss` files are strictly forbidden.
- **NPC Trainer Visual Lifecycle & GSAP State Choreography**:
  - **Step 1 (Entrance & Dialogue)**: The trainer slides in from off-screen right (`x: '150%'` to `x: '0%'`, `y: 0`, `scale: 1`, `transformOrigin: 'bottom center'`) and MUST remain stationary at center stage (`p2Pos`, `x: 0, y: 0`) at full size (`:w="baseEntitySizeEnemy"`, `scale: 1.0`) while displaying dialogue speech bubbles during `SEARCH_PHASE` / `SHOW_DIALOGS`. Dialogue speech bubbles (`BattleTrainerSpeechBubble` / `.battle-dialog-layer`) MUST be layered above the atmosphere layer (`AtmosphereLayer`) sharing the combat HUD z-index (`calc(var(--z-base) + 30)`) to remain unobstructed by weather particles or fog. Premature snapping to background retreat coordinates is strictly forbidden.
  - **Step 2 (Combat Confirmation & Retreat)**: Upon confirming combat ("LUCHAR"), the trainer retreats to their background standing position (`x: 300px, y: -135px, scale: 0.75`, `transformOrigin: 'bottom center'`) via GSAP (`triggerTrainerRetreat` using `TRAINER_RETREAT_X_OFFSET_PX`, `TRAINER_RETREAT_Y_OFFSET_PX`, `TRAINER_RETREAT_SCALE = 0.75`) and stays anchored there for the duration of `ACTIVE_BATTLE`.
  - **Step 3 (Standing In-Combat Entity & Mutual Exclusion)**: Standing trainer entities (`standingTrainerRef` / `.standing-trainer.enemy-trainer`) MUST use `:w="baseEntitySizeEnemy"` and CSS `transform: scale(0.75); transform-origin: bottom center;` to maintain seamless 0px/0% continuity with the GSAP retreat timeline. Presentation entities (`trainerRef`) and standing combatant entities MUST be strictly mutually exclusive via `:show-standing-trainers` and FSM states to prevent duplicate rendered instances.
  - **Step 4 (Battle Conclusion & Off-Camera Exit)**: Upon battle completion (victory, defeat, or fleeing), `triggerTrainerExit` animates the trainer moving off-screen to the right (`x: +=600px`).
  - **Step 5 (Team Rocket Flee Lore)**: Defeated or fleeing Team Rocket grunts (`trainerArchetype === 'rocket' | 'grunt'`) trigger the `flee` sound and rapid escape upon exit.
- **Battle Pixel-Art Integrity & Sprite Rendering**:
  - All animated battle entity containers and sprite layers (trainers, Pokémon combatants, atmosphere wrappers, status effect overlays, and sprite animators) MUST explicitly declare strict pixel-art rendering properties:

    ```scss
    image-rendering: -webkit-optimize-contrast !important;
    #{"image-rendering"}: crisp-edges !important;
    image-rendering: pixelated !important;
    -ms-interpolation-mode: nearest-neighbor !important;
    ```

  - Entity images MUST consume `@include sprite-render;` (from `src/styles/core/_gpu.scss`), which provisions dedicated GPU layer containment and pixel-art rendering.
  - Never use `@include pixelated;` for image/sprite rendering (which is strictly a typography/font mixin from `_layout.scss` for font smoothing and `font-pixel`).
- **GSAP Clock Standard & Zero-Timer Mandate (`gsapSleep`)**: All battle component animations, delays, transitions, and pauses (such as defeat screens, stage stabilization, and catch sequences) MUST be sequenced exclusively via GSAP (`gsapSleep` from `@/logic/utils/gsapHelpers` or `ctx.animations.awaitTween(...)`). Native `sleep(...)` and `setTimeout(...)` are strictly forbidden. This guarantees instant time-scaling in simulations (`gsap.globalTimeline.timeScale(...)`) while maintaining smooth 1x visual playback.
- **PvP Visual Turn Timer Clock Presentation Delay (`PvPTurnTimerClock.vue`)**: Mounted within floating arena controls in `BattleArenaView.vue`. It MUST remain conditionally suppressed during initial trainer entry and encounter presentations (`v-if="battleStore.uiConfig.showTurnTimer && !battleStore.isIntroAnimating"`). It displays an SVG circular progress ring, remaining digital seconds (`#pvp-timer-number`), AFK strike badges (`#pvp-afk-strikes`), and triggers a crimson warning pulse when time is <= 15s. During reconnection windows, displays reconnection label and countdown.
- **No-Bag Controls Flexbox Layout & Spacer Standard (`BattleArenaControls.vue`)**: In PvP matches, spectator mode, replay, or encounters where bag usage is disabled (`uiConfig.allowBag === false`), the controls container `.battle-controls-layout` MUST maintain `justify-content: flex-start` and render a fallback spacer `<div v-else class="quick-shortcut-zone zone-spacer" aria-hidden="true" />` styled with `flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 0; border: none; pointer-events: none;`. This absorbs residual container width on the right edge and keeps the moves grid (`.controls-content`) centered adjacent to the team panel, strictly preventing two-element `space-between` from pulling moves to the far right screen edge.
- **Tactical Battle Replay Theater (`BattleTacticalReplayer.vue`, `BattleReplayModal.vue`)**: Step-by-step turn inspection interface with authentic Fog-of-War. Reconstructs spectator battle states through step-by-step application of choice streams, hiding unrevealed items, abilities, and moves until used in combat. Controls include play/pause, restart, next-turn stepping, BattleCode copy, and responsive HUD layout.
- **UID-Based Element Binding & Selection Parity**: All interactive combatant and bench cards MUST bind unique HTML `id` attributes and canonical UID attributes (`data-pokemon-uid="${uid}"`) to ensure deterministic selection across UI replayers and Playwright tests without text-matching ambiguities.
- **Combat Quick Team Card & Grid Dimensions (`BattleQuickTeam.vue`, `BattleArenaControls.vue`)**:
  - The quick team panel height is strictly bounded by `.battle-controls-layout` (192px available height).
  - Cards inside `.quick-team-grid` MUST calculate their height from available container height minus symmetrical vertical padding: $192\text{px} - 8\text{px} = \mathbf{184\text{px}}$ (`height: 184px !important; min-height: 184px !important; max-height: 184px !important; padding: 4px 6px;`). Symmetrical padding (4px top, 4px bottom) guarantees that the active card halo (`box-shadow`) is never clipped.
  - In `.quick-team-grid`, `grid-auto-rows` MUST explicitly match the card height (`184px`). Setting `grid-template-rows: 1fr; height: 100%` or `min-height: 0` is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN because wrapping to 2+ rows on narrow viewports squashes row 1 into unusable thin strips.
  - In `.quick-shortcut-zone.zone-team`, overriding with `overflow: visible !important;` is STRICTLY PROHIBITED. The container MUST preserve `overflow-y: auto !important; overflow-x: hidden !important;` alongside `@include smooth-scroll;` so users can scroll vertically to view wrapped rows when screen width decreases.
- **Combat FX Filter Isolation Mandate (Atmosphere vs Weather Decoupling)**: Environmental day/night lighting filters (`var(--atmosphere-filter)`) MUST be applied strictly and exclusively to physical arena actors and props (Pokémon sprites, trainer sprites, physical Pokéball body spheres, and ground shadows). Placing visual FX (such as catch sparkles `.catch-success-sparkles`, status particles in `PVStatusFX`, auras in `PVAuraFX`, smoke, and ground hazards) inside containers carrying `--atmosphere-filter` is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to prevent CSS filter cascading and nighttime darkening. All combat FX layers MUST explicitly declare `filter: var(--weather-filter, none);` to remain immune to the time-of-day cycle while dynamically responding to in-combat weather conditions (rain, sandstorm, harsh sun, snow).
- **Combatant Template Binding Decoupling**: In `BattleArenaView.vue` and `BattleCombatant.vue`, combatant presentation flags (`:is-silhouette`, `:hidden`, `:is-emerging`) MUST NOT rely on synchronous identity checks against transient pointers (such as `p.uid === activeEnemyData?.uid`) that may evaluate to `null` during FSM resets or search transitions. Presentation props must be derived deterministically from stable combatant seat flags and battle mode states to eliminate 1-frame unmasked sprite flickers.
- **Critical Capture Arcade Banner & Visual FX Standard**:
  - The critical capture alert MUST render as large floating arcade typography: `font-size: 24px`, `font-weight: 900`, pure white `#ffffff`, uppercase, with a solid 2px black text outline (`@include text-outline(#000, 2px)`).
  - Pill containers, dark background boxes, borders, and modal wrappers for the critical capture text are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (`background: transparent; border: none; box-shadow: none; padding: 0;`).
  - The banner and radial golden sparkles MUST support standalone presentation (`.standalone` anchored at `top: 15%`), allowing full visual and audio verification (`criticalThrow` 8-bit whistle) even when no physical Pokéball is currently active on screen.
- **Combat Animation Playground & Capture Debug Protocol**:
  - The effects debug panel (`DebugAudioAnimTab.vue` / `battleDebug.ts`) MUST expose both full capture simulation routines (`CAPTURA NORMAL (COMPLETA)` with 3 shakes and `CAPTURA CRÍTICA (COMPLETA)` with whistle, banner, and exactly 1 shake) that automatically reset the Pokémon after 1.5s for seamless replayability, as well as standalone phase buttons (`FX BANNER CRÍTICO + AUDIO`, `RAYO ATRAPAR`, `SACUDIDA`, `ÉXITO`).
- **Pokémon Spritesheet In-Combat Animation Loop Speedup (30% Canonical Speed Standard)**:
  - The base playback speed for animated Pokémon combatant spritesheet loops (`useBattleCombatantSpriteLoop.ts`) MUST adhere to canonical constants in `@/logic/constants/animations`: `POKEMON_SPRITE_IDLE_FPS = 10.4` (8 *1.30) and `POKEMON_SPRITE_VARIATION_FPS = 13` (10* 1.30), accelerating idle and variation frame stepping by 30%.
  - This speedup applies *exclusively* to the spritesheet horizontal frame translation loop. It MUST NEVER alter organic GSAP breathing tweens, floating offsets, or combat action transitions.
  - Dev tools (e.g. `ShadowEditorCard.vue`) and preview cards MUST strictly consume `POKEMON_SPRITE_IDLE_FPS` rather than defining disconnected or hardcoded animation durations, ensuring 1:1 speed parity between design editors and combat arenas.
- **Standing Trainer Entity Spatial Standardization & Ground Calibration**:
  - Standing player trainer entities in `BattleTrainerEntities.vue` (`standingPlayerWidth`, `standingPlayerX`, `standingPlayerY`) MUST maintain scaled aspect-ratio bounding boxes derived from `baseEntitySizePlayer = 225` and `objectScale = 2` (450px height, 178px width based on 65:165 aspect ratio).
  - Standing player vertical alignment MUST use `STANDING_PLAYER_Y_OFFSET_PX = 55` relative to the bottom safe zone anchor (`SAFE_ZONE_Y + SAFE_ZONE_HEIGHT - ENTITY_SIZE_PLAYER + 55`) to eliminate floating, grounding the trainer's feet on the battlefield line while providing ample clearance beneath top HUD elements.
  - In-combat trainer sprites MUST anchor their feet at `TRAINER_GROUND_Y = '100%' as const` with shadow anchor `TRAINER_SHADOW_FEET_Y = 1.0`, ensuring the trainer's feet and shadow touch the baseline of the virtual entity box without mid-air floaters.
- **Combat Shadow Visual Inspection Mode (`isSolidShadows`)**:
  - The combat shadow renderer in `BattleCombatantShadow.vue` supports an inspection toggle `isSolidShadows: boolean`.
  - When enabled, it replaces semi-transparent blurred ellipses with high-contrast, fully opaque crimson geometry (`#ff0033`, `opacity: 1`, `box-shadow: 0 0 6px #ff0033`).
  - This allows developers and designers to verify the precise ground contact line, geometric anchor, and perspective footprint of combatants and trainers against stage lines without visual ambiguity.
- **Debug Panel Action Decomposition (`useDebugBattleActions.ts`)**:
  - In `DebugPanel.vue` and related debug suites, battle mutation actions and event triggers MUST be decomposed into specialized composables (such as `useDebugBattleActions.ts`).
  - This separates UI layout rendering from complex simulation triggers (force capture, weather toggles, state resets), upholding the 500-line modularity rule.
- **Canonical Seat Lifecycle & The Golden Rule of Seats (`BattleArenaView.vue`, `useBattleCombatants.ts`)**:
  - Seats 1 and 2 are strictly empty (`null` / `[]`) during `CONTEXT_SETUP` and `INITIALIZING`.
  - In wild encounters, Seat 2 is populated at `SEARCH_PHASE` (`PREPARATION`) so that `CombatGrass` (bushes), silhouette shaders, and ground shadows render seamlessly, performing the emergence jump in `ENTRY_ANIM`. Seat 1 is occupied before `POKEMON_CALL` with `scale(0)` damping (`animState = 'releasing'`).
  - In trainer/gym encounters, Seat 2 remains strictly empty throughout `SEARCH_PHASE` while the trainer speaks at center stage. Both seats are populated before `POKEMON_CALL` with initial `scale(0)` damping.
  - Seats are vacated strictly after faint, capture, or retreat animations have finished 100%.

## Work Guidance

- Ensure clean decoupling and zero-warning type safety.
- **Typed Template Refs & Expose Helpers**: When extracting child SFC subcomponents that expose DOM elements via `defineExpose`, always expose typed helper methods (e.g., `getTrainerElement(): HTMLElement | null`) instead of performing double type assertions (`as unknown as HTMLElement`) in parent watchers.
- **Scoped SCSS in Battle Subcomponents**: All extracted battle UI subcomponents must explicitly link their scoped stylesheet (`src="@/styles/components/_battle-arena-view.scss"`) to share design tokens, GPU layers, and mixins without CSS duplication.
- **Combat Grass & Emergence Layering (Mandatory Relative Z-Index)**: Front combat grass (`CombatGrass layer="front"`), back bush (`.back-bush-entity`), and all battle combatants (`BattleCombatant`) MUST ALWAYS use relative z-index bindings using CSS `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) + X)` values (e.g. `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) - 5)` for back bush, `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) + 1)` for front bush when behind, `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) + 2)` for enemy Pokémon, `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) + 3)` for front bush when covering, and `calc(var(--z-map-spawns) + 4)` for player Pokémon). Hardcoding small literal numeric z-indexes (`:z-index="2"`, `:z-index="4"`) is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN because inline styles override CSS cascade rules and cause automatic fixes or refactors to place Pokémon behind environmental layers. All intermediate FSM states between emergence jump start and active battle (such as `REORDER_TEAM`) MUST keep `bushIsBehind = true` to prevent z-index flicker during async worker initializations.
- **Floating Pokémon Combat Grass Suppression Contract (`useBattleHud.ts`, `BattleArenaView.vue`)**: In search encounters, grass layers (`CombatGrass`) MUST NOT render if no enemy data is ready (`!activeEnemyData.value`) or if the enemy is floating (`enemyIsFloating = checkFloatingState(activeEnemyData.value)`). This prevents visual pop-in or sudden grass unmounting when wild flying/levitating Pokémon spawn.
- **Complete Animation Bridge Mapping**: Local component animation bridge objects (such as `localAnimations` in `BattleArenaView.vue`) MUST explicitly map and forward ALL methods exported by domain animation composables (including `handleWithdrawRequest`, `handleReleaseRequest`, and `handleCatchRequest`). Omitting exported methods causes consumer state handlers to evaluate animation requests to `undefined`, silently bypassing critical animation sequences.
- **Strict MoveTooltip Type Alignment**: MoveTooltip SFC subcomponents (`MoveTooltipDamage.vue`, `MoveTooltipStatsGrid.vue`, `MoveTooltipStatus.vue`) MUST import and use the derived `ActiveMoveDetails` type exported by `useMoveTooltip.ts` for their `activeDetails` prop, ensuring 1:1 parity with composable calculations and zero type mismatch warnings.
- **Snappy Battle Animation Timings**: In-combat hit reactions (`PLAY_DAMAGE` / `-damage`) MUST use dedicated combat damage shake durations (`0.25s`) without trailing capture rest intervals. Physical attack dashes MUST follow snappy timings (~0.36s total: 0.08s windup, 0.14s strike, 0.14s return) to guarantee responsive retro-modern combat pacing.
- **Terrain & Atmosphere Visual Decoupling**: In `BattleArenaView.vue`, visual lighting and atmospheric tinting (`useWeatherVisuals`) must be resolved via `effectiveBattleVisual` (prioritizing `fieldConditions` such as electric/psychic/misty/grassy terrains with highest precedence), whereas weather particle layers (`AtmosphereLayer`) require validated `WeatherId` values to prevent runtime render exceptions.
- **Gym & Indoor Weather/Cycle Isolation**: In `BattleArenaView.vue`, gym combats (`isGym` / `locationId === 'gym'`), PvP battles (`locationId === 'pvp'`), and indoor arenas (`isIndoors` / `isCave` / `isCrystalCave`) MUST strictly enforce constant indoor daylight (`effectiveCycle = 'day'`) and block natural outdoor map weather (`effectiveBattleVisual = 'clear'`). Weather particles (`AtmosphereLayer`) and CSS atmosphere filters (`--atmosphere-filter`, `--weather-filter`) are strictly disabled inside interior arenas unless a battle move (*Rain Dance*, *Sunny Day*, etc.) or ability (*Drizzle*, *Drought*, etc.) actively casts in-combat weather (`battle.weather.type !== 'none' && battle.weather.type !== 'clear'`).
- **Combat Tooltip Typography & Symbol Fallbacks**: All combat move tooltip components (`MoveTooltip.vue`, `MoveTooltipStatsGrid.vue`, `MoveTooltipDamage.vue`, `MoveTooltipStatus.vue`, `MoveTooltipDetails.vue`, `MoveTooltipTactical.vue`) MUST import and consume typography tokens and mixins exclusively from `_move-tooltip-shared.scss`. Special symbols missing from the primary bitmap pixel font (`'Pokemon FireRed LeafGreen'`), such as the infinity symbol (`.infinity-val`, `.infinity-emoji`) or em-dashes (`.dash-val`), MUST explicitly override `font-family` with `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif !important;`, enable antialiasing (`-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased !important;`), and scale up to 13-14px (`font-weight: 900 !important;`) to guarantee identical visual weight, stroke thickness, and vertical alignment with 8px pixel font numerals.
