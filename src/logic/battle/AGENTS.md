# Purpose

Manage the logic and assets of battle, including the FSM orchestrator, combat engine, and the Showdown simulator bridge.

## Ownership

Frontend Developers / Systems Engineers.

## Local Contracts

- Follow standard repository modularity guidelines.
- **Opponent AI Presets & Anti-Switch-Loop Governance**: Opponent AI in battle executes via `HeuristicAI` (with `ScriptedAI` reserved exclusively for E2E mocks). Difficulty is parametrized across 5 tiers (`novice`, `intermediate`, `tactical`, `elite`, `gym`, `rival`) derived from `trainerArchetype`. The Rival is unconditionally the apex tier (0% error rate, 85% switch aggressiveness, 1-turn cooldown). Mid-combat tactical switches are strictly gated by `hasViableSwitchCounter()` and dynamic anti-ping-pong cooldowns. Asynchronous PvP passive defense battles strictly inherit the apex `rival` AI preset. See [ai/AGENTS.md](./ai/AGENTS.md).
- **Unified Combat Engine & Mode Parameterization Mandate (Zero Duplication)**: All combat encounters across the game—including PvP, Gym battles, Trainer battles, Faction War, and Wild encounters—MUST execute 100% through the exact same canonical battle engine and UI components (`BattleArena.vue`, `BattleArenaControls.vue`, `BattleQuickTeam.vue`, `PokemonSelectionModal.vue`). Creating separate arenas, parallel turn resolution loops, mock battle components, or duplicate switch modals for specific modes is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN. Mode-specific features (such as turn timer clocks `PvPTurnTimerClock` in PvP, gym leader badges, or forfeit buttons) MUST be parameterized via clean props, flags (`battleMode`, `isPvP`, `isGym`), or extension/inheritance interfaces, ensuring zero code duplication across all modes.
- **Canonical Turn Runner SSoT (`executeCanonicalTurn`)**: All battle turn executions across all modes (PvE, Gym, PvP, Spectator, Replay) MUST execute authoritatively through `executeCanonicalTurn` in `src/logic/battle/helpers/canonicalTurnRunner.ts`. Inlining duplicate worker message dispatching or log animation handling in stores or turn modules is strictly prohibited.
- **Canonical Battle Team Coordinator & Party SSoT Mandate (`getActiveCombatTeam`)**: In any battle mode with format-constrained or custom party rosters (such as 3v3 live/asynchronous PvP, ranked matches, battle factory, or cup challenges), all switch actions, health queries, bench evaluations, and UI team panels MUST execute against `getActiveCombatTeam(ctx)` via `battleTeamCoordinator.ts` instead of directly referencing or indexing `gameStore.state.team`. Indexing the player's 6-member adventure party directly causes party desync (e.g. Alakazam in 3v3 slot 0 mapping to Marowak in adventure party slot 0).
- **Atomic Turn-End State Synchronization Mandate**: Background Showdown Web Worker message listeners and client bridge receivers (`showdownWorkerClient.ts`) MUST NEVER invoke `syncTeamsFromLastWorkerState()` mid-stream during turn resolution. Synchronizing state before or during animation playback corrupts multi-hit moves (such as Bone Rush), creating visual damage-then-heal oscillation loops. Combatant HP, status, and team synchronization MUST execute strictly once at the end of the canonical turn in `canonicalTurnRunner.ts`.
- **Competitive Combatant Sanitization & Seat Sprite FX Reset**: Registered PvP teams and bench members MUST be 100% cleansed of all non-innate volatiles, cursed states, residual faint damage, and guardian auras prior to battle serialization and registration (`saveSerializer.ts`, `orchestrator.ts`). Visual sprite components (`PVSpriteFX.vue`) MUST watch `pokeId` to cleanly tear down, reset, and purge active GSAP tweens and CSS filters whenever a new Pokémon occupies the seat, preventing visual aura and status effect bleeding across combatants.
- **Scenario-Based Arena Atmosphere & Fixed Cycle Isolation**: Natural outdoor route weather is strictly disabled in Gyms, Caves, Crystal Caves, and Indoors regardless of PvP or PvE mode. Effective lighting cycles are locked to `'day'` in Gyms and Indoors, and `'night'` in single-sprite caves, managed exclusively via `battleTeamCoordinator.ts` (`isNaturalWeatherAllowedInLocation`, `resolveEffectiveCycleForLocation`). Only active move casts (*Rain Dance*, *Sunny Day*) or weather abilities (*Drizzle*, *Drought*) can trigger weather in these environments.
- **Flinch Protocol & Elastic GSAP Animation Parity**: The Showdown log bridge protocol in `showdownBridgeItemAbility.ts` MUST explicitly handle `|-cant|...|flinch` and `'cant'` without unhandled parser warnings, increment `volatileCounters['flinch'] = 1`, and trigger the elastic GSAP flinch animation (`PLAY_FLINCH`).
- **Session Strategy Hierarchy (`src/logic/battle/session/`)**: Battle modes are encapsulated in polymorphic subclasses of `BaseBattleSession` (`PvEBattleSession`, `GymBattleSession`, `PvPBattleSession`, `SpectatorBattleSession`, `ReplayBattleSession`) created via `BattleSessionFactory`. All session classes must use explicit `.ts` relative import extensions and maintain zero runtime fallback logic.
- **PvP Dual-Perspective Seat Inversion Contract**: In multiplayer PvP, the local client's combatant MUST ALWAYS be mapped to `side: 'player'` (bottom of the screen, back sprite, bottom HUD controls) and the remote combatant to `side: 'enemy'` (top of the screen, front sprite, top HUD). The `ShowdownPerspectiveAdapter` bridges authoritative Showdown seats (`p1` and `p2`) such that Player 1 maps `p1 -> player` and `p2 -> enemy`, while Player 2 maps `p2 -> player` and `p1 -> enemy`. All protocol streams and UI requests MUST be translated through this adapter to maintain perspective integrity.
- **PvP 2-Strike AFK Timeout & Forfeit Contract**: Each PvP turn has a strict `PVP_TURN_TIMEOUT_SEC = 45` limit. If the timer reaches 0 without player input on Turn 1 (Strike 1), the engine auto-selects Move 1 and issues an AFK warning toast. If the timer reaches 0 a second time while an active strike exists (Strike 2), the battle engine triggers an immediate forfeit (`pvp_forfeit` with `reason: 'afk_forfeit'`), terminating the match and awarding victory to the active opponent. Manual action clears accumulated strikes.
- **PvP Bag Prohibition Contract**: In live PvP battles, consumable bag items (potions, revives, x-items) are strictly prohibited; the UI Bag button (`#action-bag`) MUST remain disabled. Only held items (`HeldItem`) equipped to Pokémon function during PvP.
- **Pure Domain Typing in Battle Calculations & Calculators**: Calculation helpers and mathematical functions (`calculateDamagePure`, `calculateWeatherDamageMultiplier`, `statBreakdownHelper`) MUST consume pure domain types (`PurePokemon`, `PureMove`, `PureBattleWeather`, `PureDamageOptions`) with strongly-typed identifiers. Performing defensive runtime type interrogations (`typeof move.id === 'string'`, `typeof pokemon.id === 'string'`) inside pure math functions is strictly prohibited. External dynamic inputs or CLI scripts MUST validate and guard types at the conversion boundary using `isDomainId()` or `requireDomainId()`.
- **Comprehensive Battle Controls Locking & Forced Move Routing**: The UI controls (`BattleArenaControls.vue`, `BattleMovesGrid.vue`, `BattleActionButtons.vue`, `BattleQuickTeam.vue`, `BattleQuickBag.vue`, `BattleArena.vue`) MUST maintain all elements visible while enforcing strict disabled states whenever the active Pokémon is in a locked or charging state (`isPokemonLocked(player)`). Non-permitted moves in `BattleMovesGrid.vue` are visually disabled (`isMoveDisabled = true`), leaving only the forced move clickable. Switch, Bag, Quick Team, Quick Bag, and Flee (`prevent-close` in `BattleArena.vue` and `executeFlee` in `battleFlee.ts`) are unconditionally disabled/prevented. When the player executes the enabled move, `resolvePlayerForcedMoveIndex()` and `computeP1Choice()` MUST map the selection to the active forced move and submit Showdown's canonical single-slot choice (`move 1`), strictly preventing the physical UI slot number (`move 2..4`) from being forwarded directly when Showdown is awaiting its pre-determined 2-turn/recharge resolution. All state updates in `showdownWorkerClient.ts` writing back `playerRequest` or `enemyRequest` to `battleStore.state` MUST explicitly unwrap Vue reactive references (`((battleStore.state as { value?: BattleState })?.value || battleStore.state)`), ensuring properties are persisted directly onto the `BattleState` object rather than the `RefImpl` shell.
- **Multi-Turn & Continuation Move GSAP Attack Animations**: In `showdownBridgeCore.ts`, move line parsers (`handleMoveToken`) MUST unconditionally trigger attack animations (`await store.animations.awaitTween('attack-player' | 'attack-enemy')`) on all successfully executed moves, including continuation turns with `|[from]lockedmove` (*Outrage*, *Thrash*, *Petal Dance*) and effect-called moves (`[from]Sleep Talk`, `[from]Metronome`, `[from]Dancer`). Attack animations MUST ONLY be skipped when the move explicitly missed or failed (`[miss]`, `[notarget]`, `[still]`). Skipping animations on `line.includes('[from]')` is strictly prohibited.
- **Auto-Battle Rewards Phase Delay (`AUTO_BATTLE_REWARDS_DELAY_SEC = 1.5s`)**: When `uiStore.autoBattle` is active in persistent search encounters (`wasSearching === true`), `terminateBattle` in `resolution.ts` MUST wait `AUTO_BATTLE_REWARDS_DELAY_SEC` (1.5s) using `await gsapSleep(...)` after flushing all combat logs (`await ctx.waitForLogs()`). This guarantees players have sufficient time to read battle resolution rewards before transitioning to `completeBattleFlow('search')`. Because it uses GSAP's timeline clock, the delay scales automatically under `gsap.globalTimeline.timeScale(100)` during Playwright simulations.
- **Mid-Battle Voluntary Pokémon Switch Synchronization**: Voluntary mid-battle switches in `switchWorkerTurn.ts` MUST always resolve the Web Worker instance via `getShowdownWorker()` with fallback to `window.__showdownWorker__`, throwing loud errors if unavailable (strictly prohibiting silent `if (!showdownWorker) return`). The worker executes `switch <slot>` as the p1 choice, updating `activeBattle.playerRequest` with the active Pokémon's legal move set to maintain strict parity between client UI controls and `@pkmn/sim` combat logic.
- **Showdown Worker Lifecycle & Client Resilience Mandate**: `showdownWorkerClient.ts` is the authoritative bridge to the Web Worker. Handlers such as `executeTurnInWorker` and `switchWorkerTurn` MUST enforce fail-fast verification of worker initialization (`getShowdownWorker()`), throwing descriptive errors if uninitialized. Any code change to `showdownWorkerClient.ts`, `canonicalTurnRunner.ts`, or session runners MUST be verified with real Playwright E2E battle simulations (`npm run sim:e2e:combat`), strictly prohibiting reliance on unit tests that mock the worker client.
- **Final-Turn Double Faint Victory Guard**: When a simultaneous KO occurs on the final turn of combat (e.g. Explosion, recoil, or Destiny Bond), `processPlayerFaintSequence` MUST verify whether all opponent Pokémon are fainted (`active.over || !enemyHasHealthy`). If the opponent has no healthy Pokémon left, combat MUST terminate immediately with victory (`terminateBattle(ctx, true)`) and automatically swap to the next healthy bench Pokémon via `animatePlayerAutoSwap` rather than forcing the player into `SWITCH_MENU`.
- **Capture Wobble Cycle & Zero-Timer Timing Parity**:
  - When executing Pokéball capture attempts in `battleItems.ts`, all shake animation requests dispatched to `handleShakeRequest` MUST explicitly include `{ side: 'enemy', isCapture: true }`. Omitting `isCapture: true` causes the animation engine to treat the event as a combat damage flinch (`0.05s`), bypassing the canonical Pokéball wobble sequence.
  - Capture wobble cadence MUST be orchestrated strictly via GSAP timelines (`GSAP_CAPTURE_SHAKE_ACTIVE_DUR_SEC = 0.60s` physical wobble + `GSAP_CAPTURE_SHAKE_REST_DUR_SEC = 0.40s` dramatic rest interval = 1.00s total per cycle). Using `setTimeout` or `setInterval` for inter-shake pacing is strictly prohibited under the Zero-Timer policy.
- **Canonical Critical Capture Math & Resolution Contract (`battleCatchMath.ts`)**:
  - The modified catch rate $a$ is computed pure using HP factor, ball multiplier, and status multipliers ($2.0\times$ for Sleep/Freeze, $1.5\times$ for Paralyze/Burn/Poison, $1.0\times$ none). If $a \ge 255$, the Pokémon is automatically captured without executing shake rolls.
  - Critical Capture threshold $CC = \lfloor (\min(255, a) \cdot P) / 6 \rfloor$ scales strictly against caught Pokédex count (251 species total): $0$ (<15), $0.5$ (15-49), $1.0$ (50-99), $1.5$ (100-149), $2.0$ (150-199), and $2.5$ (>=200).
  - When Critical Capture triggers, the engine performs **exactly 1 shake check** against $b = \lfloor 65535 \cdot (a / 255)^{0.25} \rfloor$. A pass results in immediate capture (`shakes: 1`, `caught: true`); a fail breaks out immediately (`shakes: 0`, `caught: false`). Standard captures execute up to 4 shake checks.
- **Showdown Side Synchronization**: When synchronizing health and statuses between the local reactive game state and the Showdown simulator (via `syncSideStates` in the worker), always map and send values in the **original team order**. The simulator's `side.pokemon` array remains in its original order throughout the battle; using active-swapped orders (e.g., `playerOrder` or `enemyOrder`) results in index mismatches, corrupting simulator state during switches or item usage and throwing `INVALID_CHOICE` exceptions.
- **Pre-turn Side State Synchronization (`syncSidePokemon`)**: When syncing client HP/statuses before turn execution in `showdownSyncHelper.ts`, never invoke `pokemon.faint()` directly on simulator Pokemon instances. Direct invocation of `faint()` pushes into `faintQueue` and clears `pokemon.switchFlag = false`, which corrupts subsequent switch choices and triggers invalid `Can't switch: You sent more switches than Pokémon that need to switch` errors during faint replacement sequences. Fainted state must be assigned statically (`p.hp = 0; p.fainted = true; p.status = 'fnt' as ID; clearPokemonFromFaintQueue(side, p)`).

  - Future Sight and Doom Desire are **slot conditions**, NOT Pokémon volatiles. They live on `BattleState.pendingSlotEffects: PendingSlotEffect[]`.
  - The `PendingSlotEffect` interface is defined in `src/types/battle/battle.ts`. Each entry holds `{ move, side, targetSlot, turnsLeft, damage, sourceName? }`.
  - `damage` is pre-computed at cast time (15% of target's `maxHp`, minimum 10). It is stored in the effect, not re-computed on resolution.
  - The effect fires on the **Pokémon currently occupying `targetSlot`** on `side` when `turnsLeft` reaches 0 — regardless of which Pokémon was originally targeted. This matches official game behavior.
  - Do NOT add `futureSightTurns` or `futureSightDmg` to the `Pokemon` type — those fields have been removed.
  - `pendingSlotEffects` is ticked in `battleFlow.ts` during upkeep. `resetActiveBattleState` clears it to `[]` between battles.

- **Active Battle Persistence & Zero-Loss Rehydration**:
  - All properties of `activeBattle` (`trainerSprite`, `trainerArchetype`, `quote`, `wasSearching`, `participants`, `enemyTeamIndex`, `battleLogs`, `playerStages`, `enemyStages`) MUST be serialized in `ActiveBattleSerialized` / `activeBattleSchema` and fully restored upon rehydration in `orchestratorRestoreHelper.ts`.
  - In-combat action resolvers (such as `processSwitchSwapAnimations`, `executeSwitch`, and `executeTurnWithAction`) MUST defensively initialize `participants` as an empty array before evaluating `.includes()`.
  - When `activeBattle.wasSearching` is active, concluding the battle MUST invoke `completeBattleFlow('search')` to resume the wild encounter search loop without closing the arena modal.
  - **Move PP Persistence & Synchronization**: In `battleStateSync.ts`, `syncTeamHP(ctx)` MUST execute before evaluating `active.over` / terminating the battle, guaranteeing that move PP deductions are committed to `GameStore.team` and SQLite prior to exiting the combat view. Move matching across Showdown requests and local Pokémon instances must strictly use canonical Showdown `id` (`m.id === am.id`), with runtime name fallbacks strictly prohibited.
  - **Anti-Cheat Refresh & Minigame Non-Persistence**: Active battles rehydrate faithfully on F5 with exact UIDs, HP, stages, and logs. Minigames (`minigame !== null`, e.g. fishing, archaeology) are strictly excluded from `activeBattle` persistence (`saveSerializer.ts`) and are dropped on reload to prevent exploit resets, returning cleanly to `/map` search mode via `resumeSearchMode(ctx, d)`.
  - **Strict Single Battle Scope Contract (`isSingle`)**: In the battle termination pipeline (`resolution.ts`), single encounters (`isSingle`) that halt exploration and display return overlays ("VOLVER AL MAPA" / "VOLVER A GIMNASIOS") are **strictly and exclusively Gym battles (`active.isGym`) and PvP battles (`active.isPvP`)** (or explicit `persistenceMode === 'SINGLE'`). Route NPC trainers and rivals encountered during exploration/searching belong to the route exploration loop (`wasSearching`) and MUST NEVER be classified as `isSingle` nor forced to exit to the map.
  - **Battle Exit Persistence & 60-Second Principle Mandate**: Battle completion and cleanup routines (`activeBattle = null`, `terminateBattle`, `handleBattleFlowCompletion`, `syncAndPersist`, `handleBattleDefeatFlow`, `livePvPEndBattleHandler`) MUST ALWAYS use standard debounced/throttled saves (`save(false)`), adhering strictly to `SaveCoordinator`'s 60-second remote cloud throttle. Injecting `forceRemote: true` during battle exits or state cleanups is strictly prohibited to prevent database write spam on every wild or trainer encounter.
  - **Battle State F5 Rehydration & Alive Opponent Selection**: When rehydrating a persistent battle on F5 (`restoreBattleState` in `orchestratorRestoreHelper.ts`):
    1. The orchestrator MUST dynamically locate the first alive Pokémon (`p.hp > 0 && !p.fainted`) in `enemyTeam`. It is strictly prohibited to blindly restore a fainted Pokémon (such as index 0 with 0 HP) when a replacement Pokémon (such as Jolteon) was sent out earlier in the fight.
    2. If ALL enemy Pokémon in `enemyTeam` are fainted, the battle MUST be cleanly aborted (`activeBattle = null`), transitioned to `EXIT_BATTLE`, and saved locally (`save(false)`).
    3. The opponent's remaining backpack items (`enemyInventory`) MUST be cloned and preserved faithfully upon restoration (`d.enemyInventory = d.enemyInventory ? { ...d.enemyInventory } : {}`).

- **Dynamic Trainer Identity in Combat Logs**:
  - All combat announcement logs for opponent trainer actions (Pokemon calls, recalls, item usage, challenges) MUST dynamically resolve the trainer name using `${active.trainerName || 'El entrenador'}` and the `'enemy_trainer'` log source, avoiding static placeholder text.
- **Atomic Faint Replacement & Sendout Invariant**:
  - When an active combatant faints in trainer battles, the combatant assignment (`active.enemy = nextEnemy`), the sendout announcement log (`"¡${active.trainerName || 'El entrenador'} envía a ${nextEnemy.name}!"`), and the release animation trigger (`ctx.animations?.handleReleaseRequest`) MUST execute atomically and upfront during `POKEMON_CALL` $\rightarrow$ `RENDER_BALL` $\rightarrow$ `OCCUPY_SEAT`. Relying on asynchronous background worker synchronization (`syncTeamsFromLastWorkerState`) or late identity checks (`if (active.enemy?.uid !== nextEnemy.uid)`) to trigger UI logs or release animations is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN, as premature state mutation silently skips visual announcements and Poké Ball release animations.
- **Trainer Intro Canonical Event Sequence**:
  - Trainer battle initialization MUST strictly adhere to the following sequence:
    1. `FIRST_INTRO` / `TRAINER_ENCOUNTER`: Log `"¡${trainerName} te desafía!"` while the trainer sprite is rendered in the arena and `enemyCombatants` is empty (`[]`).
    2. `FIRST_INTRO` / `RETREAT_AND_FADEOUT`: Animate trainer retreat to the sidebar (`triggerTrainerRetreat`).
    3. `FIRST_INTRO` / `POKEMON_CALL`: Log `"¡${trainerName} envía a ${initialEnemy.name}!"`, execute `handleReleaseRequest`, and mount the active Pokémon into `enemyCombatants`.
  - Late or out-of-order sendout logs at the end of battle initialization are strictly prohibited.
