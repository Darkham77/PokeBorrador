# Evolution List

If looking for a specific Pokémon's evolution method, press Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on a Mac) and enter the Pokémon's name to search for it on the page. Note that evolutions for regional variants (Alolan/Galarian/Paldean forms) of Pokémon are listed separately, as some of them have different evolutions or evolution requirements than their original forms; thus, if wondering how to evolve a Galarian Meowth, you should search for Galarian Meowth rather than just Meowth.

Evolutions are split into three main types: level-up evolutions, which happen when a Pokémon has just gained a level and can be canceled if the player so desires by pressing B during the evolution; player-induced evolutions, which require the player to specifically do something to this Pokémon to evolve it immediately, which cannot be canceled; and special evolutions, introduced in Sword and Shield, triggered by other actions. This page lists all level-up evolutions first, then all player-induced evolutions, and then special evolutions. In the Pokémon Legends games, all evolutions are player-induced, as Pokémon will never start to evolve automatically without player input, but the evolution becomes available from the menu when the right conditions are met.

If an evolution in the list has something in parentheses after it, that means that the method will only evolve the Pokémon if the condition in the parentheses is also met.

- Galarian Farfetch'd into Sirfetch'd (Farfetch'd landed three critical hits in the battle)

#### Travel to Location

- Galarian Yamask into Runerigus (player travels under the bridge in the Dusty Bowl in the Wild Area, Yamask has taken at least 49 HP of damage since it last fainted or was caught)

#### Player Spins in Place

- Milcery into Vanilla Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is quick and clockwise; daytime)
- Milcery into Ruby Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is quick and counterclockwise; daytime)
- Milcery into Matcha Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is quick and clockwise; nighttime)
- Milcery into Salted Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is quick and counterclockwise; nighttime)
- Milcery into Caramel Swirl Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is >5 seconds and clockwise; daytime)
- Milcery into Ruby Swirl Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is >5 seconds and counterclockwise; daytime)
- Milcery into Lemon Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is >5 seconds and clockwise; nighttime)
- Milcery into Mint Cream Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is >5 seconds and counterclockwise; nighttime)
- Milcery into Rainbow Swirl Alcremie (Milcery is holding a Strawberry Sweet, Berry Sweet, Love Sweet, Star Sweet, Clover Sweet, Flower Sweet, or Ribbon Sweet; spin is >10 seconds and counterclockwise; dusk)

#### Interact with Object

- Kubfu into Single Strike Urshifu (interact with scroll in Tower of Darkness, 8th gen only)
- Kubfu into Rapid Strike Urshifu (interact with scroll in Tower of Waters, 8th gen only)

#### Use move a certain number of times

- Stantler into Wyrdeer (use Psyshield Bash in Agile Style twenty times (Pokémon Legends: Arceus))
- Hisuian Qwilfish into Overqwil (use Barb Barrage in Strong Style twenty times (Pokémon Legends: Arceus))

#### Take recoil damage

- White-Striped Basculin into Basculegion (at least 294 total recoil damage without fainting)
