# Purpose

Manage the logic and assets of map.

## Ownership

Frontend Developers / Systems Engineers.

## Local Contracts

- `tilesRegistry.ts`: Single source of truth for querying sliced canonical map tiles (`public/assets/tiles/`), loading partitioned manifests/categories, and catalog access with constant $O(1)$ indexing.
- `proceduralMapGenerator.ts`: Deterministic, layered overworld map generator supporting town (`town`) and wild route (`route`) modes, 9-slice autotiling, directional jumpable ledges (`CollisionType = 3`), connected urban street networks, and game entities registration (Warps & Spawns).
- `inBrowserTileProcessor.ts`: Pure TypeScript client-side tile and spritesheet processor supporting 16x16 slicing, chroma-key/empty/monochrome discard filtering, deterministic Web Crypto SHA-256 deduplication, and Mode A (Tilesets) / Mode B (Compound Structures) generation.
- `customAssetsStorage.ts`: IndexedDB persistence engine (`pokevicio_studio_assets_db`) persisting dynamically imported tiles and custom structure templates across browser sessions.
- `noise/simplexNoise.ts`: High-performance deterministic 2D Simplex Noise and Fractional Brownian Motion (FBM) generator in pure TypeScript with Mulberry32 PRNG seed permutation.
- `biomeDistributionEngine.ts`: Mathematical biome distribution engine utilizing quantile thresholding, 2-pass cellular automata relaxation, and town center preservation to map water/mountain/forest percentage sliders into organic natural masks without 1x1 orphan tiles.
- `urbanZoningEngine.ts`: Intelligent urban zoning and city generator adapting street network topologies, avenue-facing parcel extraction, civic building placement (Pokémon Center, Poké Mart, Official Gym, Oak's Lab, Plazas), residential lot density fill, and guaranteed transitable doorstep-to-avenue connectivity across 5 urban scales (Hamlet to Metropolis).
- `nodeLocalMapGenerator.ts`: Bridge generator inferring canonical urban scales, dimension footprints (36x28 to 70x50), and feature sets directly from regional graph nodes (e.g., Pallet -> Hamlet with Lab; Saffron -> Metropolis with Fountain Plaza).
- `propsPlacementEngine.ts`: Scenic props and urban furniture placement engine governing deterministic rhythmic placement of street lamps, residential mailboxes, civic signposts, plaza furniture, perimeter fences, and wildflower clusters with strict pedestrian clearance invariant protection.
- `prefabsRegistry.ts`: Single source of truth for complete, transparent GBA entity sprites (buildings, vegetation, elevation, infrastructure, and props), supporting category filtering and $O(1)$ identifier lookup for the Stamp Engine and continental world generation.
- `kantoProceduralCarver.ts`: Procedural carver and stamp engine implementing organic 2.5D mountain massifs with vertical cliff faces, modular ledge walls, and peak cones, strictly eliminating flat ground tile repetition.
- `macroBiomeSynthesizer.ts`: Low-frequency 2D Simplex/FBM macro-biome synthesizer partitioning continental land into coherent ecological regions (`temperate_plains`, `viridian_forest`, `arid_desert`, `volcanic_plateau`, `mint_highland`) with minimum region thresholds ($\ge 16\times 16$) to eliminate patchwork noise, and enforcing a mandatory 2-tile dirt perimeter containment buffer (`poke_dirt_path.png`) where arid desert or volcanic plateaus border temperate plains.
- `geologicalClusterEngine.ts`: Graph-based 8-connected Connected Component Labeling (CCL) engine clustering mountain massifs into discrete geological formations, enforcing a strict 100% single-palette invariant (`brown` Mt. Moon clay, `gray` Highland Granite, or `volcanic` Tanoby red) per continuous mountain range to prevent mosaic clashing.
- `urbanPavingEngine.ts`: Canonical urban paving and infrastructure renderer stamping asphalt avenues (`poke_road_asphalt.png`), cobblestone plazas (`poke_stone_plaza_center.png`), crosswalks, and directional perimeter curbs (`poke_curb_*`) with strict Curb Clearance directly in front of building doorsteps (`doorX, doorY + 1`) and avenue crossings.
- `wildernessVegetationEngine.ts`: Macro-biome aware wilderness, tall grass encounter fields, roadside props, and organic tree copse generator enforcing strict North-to-South Y-sorting, barren biome suppression (zero trees or tall grass in arid desert and volcanic plateaus), and ecological flora distribution (evergreen pines in mint highland, dense Viridian canopies in forests, field boulders in desert).
- `canonicalBridgeEngine.ts`: Canonical 2D GBA bridge and boardwalk autotiling engine resolving 2-to-3 cell wide water crossings into direction-aware wooden planks (horizontal planks with side railings on vertical runs; vertical planks with top/bottom beams and water pilings on horizontal runs; 90° elbow turns) based on corridor span and partner connectivity, strictly eliminating monolithic floating square platforms.
- `routePathfinding.ts`: Dedicated A* pathfinding, priority queue, ocean boundary classification, and dynamic incident edge rerouting engine for regional route networks and POI manipulation, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `routeChainTopology.ts`: Canonical route chain topology engine constructing linear Hamiltonian paths across primary POIs via TSP Nearest Neighbor + 2-opt heuristic, attaching secondary POIs as proximity stubs, and injecting secondary surf/cycling loops, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `regionalPoiCatalog.ts`: Regional POI catalog and procedural POI generator defining canonical Kanto templates and procedural generator for arbitrary POI counts, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `archipelagoGenerator.ts`: Procedural archipelago island generator stamping 3-5 organic maritime islands with Voronoi cellular modulation, beach buffers, and navigation channels, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `transitableGridEngine.ts`: Nature-first transitable grid calculation engine dilating active route paths and bridges, enforcing hermetic checkpoint gatehouse doorways with impassable lateral flanks, and managing compact stair landings.
- `canvasImageLoader.ts`: Canonical 2D Canvas tile & prefab asset loader, cache manager, and URL resolver for the Canvas Tile Renderer.
- `canvasLandmarkRenderer.ts`: Dedicated rendering engine for regional POI landmarks, architectural structures, cave entrances, port docks, cargo dressing, and route gatehouses, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `continentMountainEngine.ts`: Procedural multi-tier mountain massif elevation scaling engine (Tiers 1 to 4), concentric Chebyshev clearance cushioning guaranteeing zero cardinal drops > 1 tier, orphan cliff wall pruning, and dynamic multi-tier stair discovery, strictly satisfying the 1000-line SRP architecture limit.
- `pokemonGraphTopology.ts`: Abstract Pokémon topology graph generator implementing the 7 canonical design rules (3-5 independent cycles, dead-end starter and league, 8 gyms, coastal port, wormholes, degree constraints).
- `orthogonalGraphEmbedding.ts`: Orthogonal spatial embedding engine mapping abstract topology nodes to band grids with bounded jitter and 100% orthogonal corridors (H, V, L-turns).
- `corridorPropertyEngine.ts`: Emergent corridor property engine computing continuous width, difficulty, terrain influence, ledges, water side access, and gatehouses with a no-repeat variety constraint.
- `progressionBarrierEngine.ts`: Progression barrier assignment and mathematical anti-softlock simulation engine verifying solvability across 0 to 8 badges with zero softlocks.
- `cityVignetteRegistry.ts`: Canonical $O(1)$ environmental vignette catalog declaring 10 modular micro-vignette blueprints (`fisherman_wharf`, `berry_orchard`, `cliff_lookout`, `street_bazaar`, `civic_fountain_square`, `harbor_pier_dock`, `residential_culdesac`, `sacred_shrine`, `cargo_depot`, `botanical_garden`), environmental prerequisites, and strict clearance footprints.
- `cityGeneSynthesizer.ts`: Continuous procedural gene synthesizer deriving 6 continuous urban traits (`canopyIntegration`, `waterFeatureType`, `elevationPolicy`, `urbanDensity`, `streetPattern`, `vignetteBudget` from 2 to 10) from seed, terrain, and POI node roles.
- `organicCityEngine.ts`: Organic procedural city and settlement layout engine replacing rigid 4-quadrant grids with dynamic vignette budgeting, asymmetric district packing, doorstep clearance invariants, contextual road materials (`dirt`, `paved`, `asphalt`, `stone`, `wood`, `brick`), and guaranteed doorstep-to-street connectivity.
- `routeGateBarrierEngine.ts`: Route gatehouse checkpoint barrier sealing and multi-source BFS anti-bypass verification engine guaranteeing hermetic checkpoint route corridors with impassable lateral flanks.
- `routeLedgeEngine.ts`: Canonical 1-way jumpable ledge generator (`poke_ledge_jump.png`) with 3-piece autotiling (`poke_ledge_left.png`, `poke_ledge_jump.png`, `poke_ledge_right.png`) on route corridors, enforcing a $\ge 2$-cell unblocked flanking bypass corridor to guarantee zero softlocks.
- `progressionObstacleEngine.ts`: Procedural HM progression obstacle generator placing Cut trees (`tree_cuttable.png`, Badge 2) and Strength boulders (`poke_boulder_large.png`, Badge 4) on secondary route detours and shortcuts without obstructing primary path transit.
- `microVignetteEngine.ts`: Environmental micro-vignette generator and orphan path tile sweeper discovering unallocated $\ge 3\times 3$ grass clearings to embed thematic scenic vignettes (`berry_orchard`, `civic_fountain`, `explorer_camp`, `botanical_garden`), and pruning isolated stray path cells.
- `continentGenerator.ts`: Macro-regional continental procedural world generator synthesizing 256x256 / 320x320 overworld maps, coordinating geological sculpting, hydrography, biomes, mountain massifs, regional routes, and settlement placements under the 1000-line SRP architectural limit.
- `continentSculptingEngine.ts`: Continental landmass sculpting, graph dilation, perimeter beach distribution, orphan tile cleaning, and inland freshwater lake carving sub-engine.
- `geologicalAutotilingEngine.ts`: Geological autotiling compilation engine translating raw continental biomes and elevation matrices into authentic GBA terrain, autotiled cliff faces, sandy shorelines, and water bodies.

## Work Guidance

- Ensure clean decoupling and zero-warning type safety.
- Mountain Generation Mandate: Mountains must never be rendered as flat repeating ground tiles. All elevated terrain must be bounded by authentic GBA cliff faces (`cliff_face`, `cliff_top`, `corners`) facing down/outwards and capped with natural rock cones (`poke_cliff_cone_brown` / `poke_cliff_cone_gray`) and caves at road crossings.
- Massif Palette Assignment: Each mountain massif or regional cluster must be assigned a coherent dominant rocky palette (Brown clay/earth style Mt. Moon vs. Gray volcanic style Mt. Ember) derived pseudo-randomly from the world seed, ensuring matching cliff faces, corners, and cones.
- Asset Deduplication: Mountain extraction scripts must employ hybrid segmentation (discrete prefabs + modular 32x32/16x16 cliff wall parts) backed by deterministic perceptual/SHA-256 hash deduplication.
- Dynamic Catalog Consumption Mandate: Urban architecture and scenic prop generators must NEVER hardcode fixed sprite lists. They MUST query the live `PrefabsRegistry` category collections (`buildings`, `vegetation`, `props`, `elevation`), selecting structures through stochastic weighted sampling so any future additions to `manifest.json` are instantly utilized without code modification.
- Urban Archetype Variety: Settlements must roll stochastic urban archetypes (residential hamlets, commercial crossroads, fortified gym cities, coastal/rural villages) using dynamic footprint packing, varied plaza geometries, asymmetrical fence perimeters, and diverse house color schemes.
- Dense Forest Clustering Mandate: Vegetation must NEVER be rendered as an evenly spaced orchard or point matrix. Trees must cluster compactly (24-28px spacing) to form dense, contiguous, overlapping canopy walls flanking route corridors, punctuated by organic groves in open clearings. The generator must dynamically mix all available catalog vegetation (`poke_tree_poke`, `poke_tree_pine_small`, `bush_round`, `poke_berry_bush`, `poke_flowers_red`, `poke_tree_cuttable`).
- Organic Coastal Transitions: Shorelines must eliminate artificial straight or sinusoidal borders. Low elevation shorelines must feature natural sand beaches (1-2 tiles) and coves between land and water, while high elevation coastal terrain must feature sea cliffs plunging directly into deep water.
- Canonical Building Scale Mandate: All building prefabs in `public/assets/prefabs/buildings/` must strictly use Pokémon Essentials 32px/tile standard scale (KANTO_TILE_SIZE = 32), matching the world terrain grid. Building widths and heights MUST be exact multiples of 32px (e.g., Pokémon Center: 160x160 px / 5x5 tiles; Poké Mart: 128x128 px / 4x4 tiles; Oak Lab: 224x192 px / 7x6 tiles; Standard Houses: 160x160 px / 5x5 tiles; Gyms: 192x160 px / 6x5 tiles). Heuristic cropping, sliced doors/thresholds, and 16px GBA half-scale building registrations are strictly forbidden.
- Settlement Clearing Scale Mandate: World carver clearing radii must scale adaptively with urban hierarchy (Hamlets/Rural ~8.5-9.5 tiles, Port ~10.5 tiles, Gym Bastions ~12-13 tiles, Metropolis ~15-16 tiles) ensuring at least 2-3 transitable tiles of breathing room around 32px-scaled structures for dirt footpaths, gardens, and perimeter fencing without collision with the forest wall.
- Regional Settlement Urban Footprint Contract: In macro-regional generation (128x128+), settlement footprints must accommodate full 32px GBA civic buildings without collisions or overlapping: Metropolis (16x14 tiles: Gym 6x5, Center 5x5, PokéMart 4x4, 3 houses, cobblestone plaza and lamps); City (12x10 tiles: Center 5x5, PokéMart 4x4, 2 houses, plaza); Town (8x8 tiles: Center 5x5 or rustic civic building, 2 houses, village plaza).
- Hybrid Urban Typology & Doorstep Clearance Contract: Settlement layouts must adhere strictly to their archetype: 'metropolis' uses an orthogonal street grid with paved avenues and street lamps; 'city' uses a central paved plaza with perimeter buildings facing inward; 'town' uses organic rural clusters on natural grass with dirt paths, gardens and fences. All buildings MUST guarantee that their front doorstep tile remains 100% clear of obstacles (zero lamps, trees, or fences blocking entrances).
- Settlement Gateway & Internal Street Connection Contract: Regional A* routes must terminate at perimeter gateway entrance points on the nearest outer edge of the settlement footprint, allowing the settlement's internal paved streets/dirt paths to smoothly channel transit to building doors without cutting destructively across urban lots.
- Zero Full-Sheet Fallback Mandate: Rendering engines (`kantoTileEngine.ts`) must NEVER draw multi-tile composite sheets (such as `bridges.png`) directly onto individual tile cells. All infrastructure stamps must resolve to verified 32x32 tiles (`poke_boardwalk_planks.png`).
- Manifest Catalog Purity Mandate: `manifest.json` and `prefabsRegistry.ts` must strictly contain discrete, single-entity GBA game assets. Multi-tile tilesets, compound texture atlases, full composite sheets (like LPC 512x512 sheets), and raw terrain fragments (such as `dirt.png`) are strictly prohibited from registration in the prefabs catalog.
- Pokémon Essentials RMXP Autotiling Mandate: All dynamic surface terrain transitions (water bodies, ponds, dirt paths, cobblestone plazas, mountain cliffs, and tall grass) MUST be governed by the standard 48-tile RMXP autotiling engine (`resolveRmxpSubtiles` in `kantoTileEngine.ts`) over 96x128 px autotile sheets in `public/assets/essentials/autotiles/`. Ad-hoc manual 9-slice lookups, flat color fallback fills (such as `#64748b` gray slabs), and non-standard LPC layouts are strictly forbidden in runtime rendering.
- Single Source of Truth Essentials Asset Pipeline: All official 32px overworld assets (prefabs and autotiles) MUST be managed via `public/assets/essentials/manifest.json` and generated or updated via `npm run map:essentials:import`. Manual, unstructured file dumping into arbitrary folders is prohibited.
- Mountain-City Safety Clearance Mandate: Mountain massifs and plateaus must never overlap settlement clearings or route corridors. All plateau generations (`raisePlateau`, `carveMountainChains`, `carveProceduralMountainChains`) MUST enforce a strict minimum clearance buffer of >= 4 tiles (128px) from all building footprints and road corridors (`OCCUPANCY_FLAGS.BUILDING | OCCUPANCY_FLAGS.ROAD`).
- Omnidirectional 4-Way Cliff Autotiling Mandate: Mountain plateaus must never terminate in abrupt flat borders. All plateau perimeters MUST be bounded by authentic GBA cliff faces, walls, and corners across all 4 cardinal directions (North rim, South face, East wall, West wall, and all 4 exterior/interior corners: `poke_cliff_brown_top`, `poke_cliff_brown_face`, `poke_cliff_brown_left`, `poke_cliff_brown_right`, `poke_cliff_brown_corner_*`).
- Organic 64x64px Canopy Forest Mandate: Forest massifs must NEVER be rendered as a single-tile 32x32 repeating point matrix or wallpaper pattern. Overworld terrain ground must remain continuous natural grass autotile (`CELL_BIOME.GRASS`), while trees MUST be placed in Layer 4 as discrete 64x64px GBA tree prefabs (`tree_poke`, `tree_oak_clean`) clustered in dense perimeter boundary walls and organic clearings with tall grass patches.
- Project Schema Versioning Mandate: Saved continental project state in browser storage (`localStorage`) MUST enforce strict schema versioning (`pokevicio_continent_projects_v2`). Outdated or legacy cached project profiles from earlier engine versions must be automatically migrated or refreshed upon store initialization, ensuring developers and players always experience current engine code without manual cache clearing.
- 3-Piece Ledge Autotiling Mandate: Jumpable ledges (`poke_ledge_jump`) MUST dynamically resolve to 3-piece autotile parts (`poke_ledge_left` rounded cap, `poke_ledge_mid` continuous body, `poke_ledge_right` rounded cap) based on horizontal neighbor presence, eliminating cut-off edges.
- 9-Slice Corner Priority Mandate: In procedural path and boundary generation, 4-way outer corners (`topLeft`, `topRight`, `bottomLeft`, `bottomRight`) MUST be evaluated before single cardinal borders (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`) to preserve continuous curves, elbows, and rounded bends.
- Zero Early-Return Autotile Bypass Mandate: Canvas tile rendering engines must never bypass RMXP autotiling via single-tile early returns (`poke_dirt_path.png`), ensuring all terrain transitions display seamless anti-aliased fringes.
- Canonical Mountain Massif Formations Mandate: Mountains must NEVER be carved as geometric rectangles, bounding-box loops (`gy = 2..7, gx = 4..W-4`), or hollow corrals. Elevated topography must be composed of organic natural mountain massifs with variable-depth terraces, rounded promontories, multi-tier ledges, authentic stairwells, and cave entrances derived from canonical GBA formations (Mt. Moon style).
- Ridge Noise Mountain Ranges Contract: Continental mountain relief in `continentGenerator.ts` MUST be generated using modulated 2D Ridge Noise (`1.0 - |noise|`) to form 2-3 elongated, sinuous mountain chains separated by a broad central habitable plain and river valleys, strictly eliminating monolithic central radial mountain domes. Interior concave pits and orphan U/L wall fragments without closed terraces or stairs are strictly forbidden and must be pruned.
- Cellular Automata & Simplex Noise Mountain Topology Contract: Mountain ranges and massifs in `kantoProceduralCarver.ts` MUST be generated exclusively through 2D Simplex Noise thresholding and Cellular Automata contour smoothing, producing natural spurs, organic bays, and multi-tier plateaus (Tier 1 base, Tier 2 summit) connected by authentic stairways and cave entrances. Hardcoded rectangular bounding loops (`gy = A..B, gx = C..D`) are strictly prohibited.
- Organic Forest Canopy Clustering Mandate: Forests and perimeter tree walls must NEVER be stamped as a rigid 2x2 coordinate matrix (`gx += 2, gy += 2`) or wallpaper grid. Vegetation MUST cluster into organic canopy masses with natural depth using staggered brick overlap (32px vertical and horizontal offsets), interlocking canopies to form dense, contiguous foliage walls flanking route corridors and mountain perimeters with irregular silhouette clearings, mimicking authentic GBA Route 1, Route 4, and Viridian Forest layouts.
- Cliff-Tree Occupancy Safety Buffer Mandate: The regional world generator (`kantoRegionalGenerator.ts`) MUST enforce an exclusion buffer of at least 1 horizontal tile and 2 vertical tiles around all cliff boundaries (`OCCUPANCY_FLAGS.CLIFF` / `CELL_BIOME.MOUNTAIN_DIRT`) when placing vegetation. Tree trunks or canopies overlapping cliff walls, tops, or corners are strictly forbidden.
- Organic Biome Vegetation Zoning Mandate: Vegetation species in forest walls and perimeter clearings MUST be distributed via organic regional biome heuristics and continuous noise transitions. Artificial coordinate threshold splits (such as hardcoded `gy < 36` or `gx <= 30` box bounds) are strictly prohibited. Mountain and high elevation zones (elev >= 2) use alpine conifers/pines (`poke_tree_pine_small`), while valley basins use canonical GBA oaks (`poke_tree_oak_clean`), transitioning smoothly via continuous Simplex noise dithering across biome frontiers.
- Route Corridor & Transition Belt Contract: Route paths must maintain a 1-2 cell clear transition buffer flanking both sides to accommodate roadside props (wooden fences, red/blue flowers) and canonical tall grass encounter patches (3x3 to 5x5). The remaining wilderness space between route corridors and mountain cliffs / ocean coasts must be filled with dense tree walls (GBA oaks in plains, pines near mountains) to frame the pathways.
- Zero Grass Under Mountain Plateau Mandate: The tile rendering engine (`kantoTileEngine.ts`) and preview renderers MUST NEVER render base grass (`poke_grass_plain.png`) under mountain cells (`CELL_BIOME.MOUNTAIN_DIRT`). The base underlay and interior of all elevated plateaus MUST be 100% solid canonical rock plateau texture (`poke_cliff_${pal}_plateau_rock.png`).
- Canonical 2-Tile Elevation Cliff Wall Mandate: In canonical GBA Pokémon (Route 3, Route 4, Route 23), south-facing cliff walls are strictly 2 tiles tall (Row 1: top rounded ledge rim / remate; Row 2: vertical textured cliff face / bottom edge). Autotilers and map carvers MUST strictly treat mountain elevations as multi-tile vertical structures, ensuring that elevated plateaus meet the lower terrain through a 2-tile high vertical face with authentic GBA stairs and cave entrances, never an isolated flat 1-tile boundary.
- Hybrid GBA Puzzle Map Architecture Mandate: Overworld route and mountain generation must operate on a hybrid jigsaw puzzle paradigm combining clean procedural terraced plateaus (Tier 1 valley ground, Tier 2 walkable terraces with stairs/items, Tier 3+ solid north barrier mountain wall) with canonical 16px/32px GBA prefabs (cave entrances seamlessly integrated into cliff walls, Pokémon Center clearings, and jumpable ledges).
- Mountain Terraces vs Northern Massif Barrier Contract: Northern massif elevation (Tier 3+) serves as the impassable boundary wall of the region framing the cave entrance, while lower terraces (elevation 2) feature walkable mountain ground/dirt connecting via authentic stone stairs for exploratory routing, items, and shortcuts.
- Mt. Moon / Route 4 Canonical Composition Contract: The approach to Mt. Moon must feature a clear open clearing to the west housing the Pokémon Center with perimeter breathing room and a southward connector path, framed to the north by the 2-tile tall canonical brown cliff wall seamlessly embedding the cave mouth archway and adjacent stone stairs rising to the upper mountain terrace.
- Strict 2-Tile South Cliff Autotile Invariant: South-facing cliff walls must strictly span 2 vertical tiles (Row Y-1: cliff top rim `_top`; Row Y: cliff vertical face `_face`). Cave mouths and stone stairs must be flush-embedded into these exact 2-tile cliff structures, seamlessly replacing the wall tiles at their footprint. Lateral borders of route corridors must be framed by continuous dense tree walls preventing off-grid escapement.
- Route 4 Canonical Interactive Pack Contract: Mt. Moon approach and Route 4 corridors must feature eastward one-way jumpable ledges (CollisionType = 3), tall grass encounter patches framed by paths, decorative ground boulders, and regional signposts, mirroring official GBA Route 4 geometry.
- Pre-Cropped Tile Exclusivity Mandate: All procedural generation and map assembly logic must strictly consume verified, pre-cropped tiles and prefabs from `public/assets/tiles/` and `public/assets/prefabs/`. Dynamic or on-the-fly slicing from raw sheets during map generation tasks is strictly forbidden. If an asset is missing, fail fast and alert the user per `@/map-tile-catalog`.
- Macro-Biome Spatial Coherence Mandate: Temperature and moisture noise fields must use low spatial frequencies (`scale <= 0.02`) to generate vast, contiguous ecological macro-regions ($\ge 16\times 16$ tiles) eliminating patchwork noise. Arid desert and volcanic plateau biomes must be bounded by mountains or water; when bordering temperate plains, a mandatory 2-cell dirt containment corridor (`poke_dirt_path.png`) must buffer the transition.
- Geological Mountain Palette Invariant Mandate: Each discrete mountain massif identified via 8-connected Connected Component Labeling must be assigned a single, continuous geological palette (`brown`, `gray`, or `volcanic`) derived from the world seed, strictly prohibiting mixed-palette cliffs within the same mountain cluster.
- Urban Paving & Curb Clearance Mandate: Urban vehicular streets must use canonical asphalt (`poke_road_asphalt.png`) and pedestrian zones must use cobblestone plaza tiles (`poke_stone_plaza_center.png`), strictly eliminating flat white slabs. Directional curb tiles (`poke_curb_*`) must be stamped between sidewalks and avenues, with mandatory clearance (curb omitted or dropped) at building doorways (`doorX, doorY + 1`) and crosswalks.
- Dynamic Asset Loader URL Sanitization Invariant: Asset registries and loaders (`TilesRegistry`, `PrefabsRegistry`) consuming parameterized or variable URLs MUST explicitly sanitize input to guarantee local host containment (`url.startsWith('/')` and `!url.includes('..')`) to neutralize SSRF (CWE-918) and directory traversal risks before executing `fetch()`.
- Canonical GBA Bridge & Water Landmark Architecture Mandate: Bridges over water bodies, canals, and bays MUST be generated as narrow walking corridors (2 to 3 cells wide) matching Pokémon FireRed/LeafGreen Route 11, Route 12, and Route 24. Monolithic floating square wooden floors are strictly prohibited. Planks must strictly run perpendicular to the walking direction with authentic wooden railings and water pilings.
