**Weather Sync-Up** (Japanese: **天気変化時Ｂ技威力↑** _Buddy Move Power Boost When Weather Change_) is a [skill](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Skill_(Masters)> 'Skill (Masters)') in [Pokémon Masters EX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Masters_EX 'Pokémon Masters EX') .

Contents

## Effect

The user's [sync moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_move 'Sync move') are powered up if any [weather](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Masters)> 'Weather (Masters)') condition ([sun](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight 'Harsh sunlight') , [rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain 'Rain') , [hail](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hail_(weather_condition)> 'Hail (weather condition)') , or [sandstorm](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sandstorm_(weather_condition)> 'Sandstorm (weather condition)') ) is active on the field of play. Higher numbered skills result in a greater boost in power according to the below formula. Currently, only Weather Sync-Up 5 is available on scoutable [sync pairs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_pair 'Sync pair') , resulting in a 50% power boost to the user's sync moves.

PowerBoost\=SkillValue×0.1
