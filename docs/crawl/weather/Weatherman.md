**Forecast** (Japanese: **てんきや** _Temperamental_) is an [Ability](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ability 'Ability') introduced in [Generation III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_III 'Generation III') . It is the [signature Ability](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Signature_Ability 'Signature Ability') of [Castform](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Castform_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Castform (Pokémon)') .

Contents

## Effect

### In battle

Depending on the [weather](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather 'Weather') , Forecast will change [Castform](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Castform_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Castform (Pokémon)') 's form, which also [changes its type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Type_change 'Type change') . Castform changes as follows:

- During [harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight 'Harsh sunlight') or [extremely harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight 'Harsh sunlight') , it will become Sunny Form, which is [Fire-type](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fire_(type)> 'Fire (type)') .
- During [hail](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hail_(weather_condition)> 'Hail (weather condition)') or [snow](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Snow 'Snow') , it will become Snowy Form, which is [Ice-type](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ice_(type)> 'Ice (type)') .
- During [rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain 'Rain') or [heavy rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain 'Rain') , it will become Rainy Form, which is [Water-type](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Water_(type)> 'Water (type)') .
- During a [sandstorm](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sandstorm_(weather_condition)> 'Sandstorm (weather condition)') , [fog](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fog 'Fog') , [strong winds](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Strong_winds 'Strong winds') , a [shadowy aura](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shadowy_aura 'Shadowy aura') , or clear weather, it will revert to its normal form, which is [Normal-type](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Normal_(type)> 'Normal (type)') .
  - It will also revert to its normal form if a Pokémon on the field has [Cloud Nine](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cloud_Nine_(Ability)> 'Cloud Nine (Ability)') or [Air Lock](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Air_Lock_(Ability)> 'Air Lock (Ability)') .

This Ability has no effect on Pokémon other than Castform.

Forecast does not change Castform's form until after [Stealth Rock](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stealth_Rock_(move)> 'Stealth Rock (move)') deals damage.

#### Generation III

If a Pokémon [transforms](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Transform_(move)> 'Transform (move)') into Castform with Forecast, it will change forms with the weather just like Castform.

If Castform or a Pokémon transformed into Castform loses Forecast, it will be stuck in its current form. When it regains Forecast, it will change to the form matching the current weather, even if it is already in the correct form (which resets its type if it had changed due to an effect like [Color Change](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Color_Change_(Ability)> 'Color Change (Ability)') ).

Forecast can be copied by [Role Play](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Role_Play_(move)> 'Role Play (move)') and [Trace](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Trace_(Ability)> 'Trace (Ability)') .

#### Generation IV

If Forecast is suppressed with [Gastro Acid](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gastro_Acid_(move)> 'Gastro Acid (move)') , it will be stuck in its current form.

Forecast cannot be copied by [Trace](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Trace_(Ability)> 'Trace (Ability)') .

#### Generation V onward

If a Pokémon [transforms](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Transform_(move)> 'Transform (move)') into Castform, the Pokémon will remain in the same form as the target Castform, regardless of the weather.

If Castform loses Forecast or Forecast is suppressed with [Gastro Acid](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gastro_Acid_(move)> 'Gastro Acid (move)') , it will revert to its normal form and not be able to change form. If Castform regains Forecast, it will change form to match the current weather, unless it would be in its normal form in the current weather.

[Role Play](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Role_Play_(move)> 'Role Play (move)') fails if the target has the Ability Forecast. [Entrainment](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Entrainment_(move)> 'Entrainment (move)') fails if the user has the Ability Forecast. Forecast cannot be copied by [Power of Alchemy](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Power_of_Alchemy_(Ability)> 'Power of Alchemy (Ability)') or [Receiver](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Receiver_(Ability)> 'Receiver (Ability)') , nor can it be traded by [Wandering Spirit](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wandering_Spirit_(Ability)> 'Wandering Spirit (Ability)') .

### Outside of battle

Forecast has no effect outside of battle.

## In other games

### [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon_series 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series')

Forecast will turn Castform into an Ice-type in [snow](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Snow 'Snow') . In [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team') , it will also turn Castform into a Water-type during foggy weather.

In Red and Blue Rescue Team, [Explorers of Time and Darkness](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Darkness 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Darkness') and [Sky](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky') , the type-changing effect of Forecast works on Pokémon other than Castform (such as if copied by [Role Play](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Role_Play_(move)> 'Role Play (move)') or [Trace](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Trace_(Ability)> 'Trace (Ability)') ), though the form-changing effect does not.
