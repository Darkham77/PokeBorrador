**Weather Surge** (Japanese: **天気変化時威力上昇** _Power Boost When Weather Change_) is a family of [skills](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Skill_(Masters)> 'Skill (Masters)') in [Pokémon Masters EX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Masters_EX 'Pokémon Masters EX') .

Contents

## Effect

The user's moves are powered up if any [weather](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Masters)> 'Weather (Masters)') condition ([sun](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight 'Harsh sunlight') , [rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain 'Rain') , [hail](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hail_(weather_condition)> 'Hail (weather condition)') , or [sandstorm](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sandstorm_(weather_condition)> 'Sandstorm (weather condition)') ) is active on the field of play. Higher numbered skills result in a greater boost in power according to the below formula. This does not affect [sync moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_move 'Sync move') or ![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/9/9f/Dynamax_icon.png/40px-Dynamax_icon.png) [max moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Max_Move 'Max Move') .

PowerBoost\=SkillValue×0.1
