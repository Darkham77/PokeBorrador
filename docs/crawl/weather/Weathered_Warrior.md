**Weathered Warrior** (Japanese: **天気変化時Ｐ技Ｂ技威力↑** _Pokémon Move and Buddy Move Power Boost When Weather Change_) is a family of [skills](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Skill_(Masters)> 'Skill (Masters)') in [Pokémon Masters EX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Masters_EX 'Pokémon Masters EX') .

Contents

## Effect

When a [weather](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Masters)> 'Weather (Masters)') condition is in effect, the power of the user's moves is increased by 10% per rank of this skill. This skill affects regular attack moves, including ![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/e/e7/Buddy_Move_Icon_Masters.png/40px-Buddy_Move_Icon_Masters.png) [buddy moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Buddy_move 'Buddy move') , as well as [sync moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_move 'Sync move') . It does not apply to ![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/9/9f/Dynamax_icon.png/40px-Dynamax_icon.png) [max moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Max_Move 'Max Move') . Currently, only Weathered Warrior 3 is available on scoutable [sync pairs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_pair 'Sync pair') .

This skill is an additive multiplier; when multiple additive skills affect the same move, they are first summed and then multiplied by the move's power.
