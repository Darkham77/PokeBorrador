**Kyogre** (Japanese: **カイオーガ** _Kyogre_) is a [recurring Pokémon character](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_recurring_wild_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_the_animated_series 'List of recurring wild Pokémon in the animated series') in [_Pokémon the Series: XY_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY 'Pokémon the Series: XY') .

### History

Kyogre was first awakened in _[Mega Evolution Special II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS02 'XYS02') _ by the presence of the [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') .

It played a significant role in _[Mega Evolution Special III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS03 'XYS03') _. [Lysandre](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lysandre 'Lysandre') first alerted [Alain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain 'Alain') and [Steven](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven_Stone 'Steven Stone') to Kyogre's appearance off the coast of [Sootopolis City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sootopolis_City 'Sootopolis City') , where it was shown in its [primal state](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') . It soon clashed with Primal Groudon over the Giant Rock. It also fought [Steven's Mega Metagross](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven%27s_Metagross "Steven's Metagross") , and was almost defeated by it until Metagross was beaten by [Mega](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution 'Mega Evolution') [Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') . Kyogre was quickly defeated by Mega Rayquaza's [Dragon Ascent](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dragon_Ascent_(move)> 'Dragon Ascent (move)') and reverted to its normal form.

It reappeared in flashbacks during _[Cloudy Fate, Bright Future!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XY092 'XY092') _ and _[The First Day of the Rest of Your Life!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XY138 'XY138') _.

### Personality and characteristics

Primal Kyogre was fixated on the [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') and was prepared to attack anyone who interfered. It was heavily reliant on [Origin Pulse](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Origin_Pulse_(move)> 'Origin Pulse (move)') in battle and used [Sheer Cold](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sheer_Cold_(move)> 'Sheer Cold (move)') to transform the ocean into an icy wasteland to its own advantage.

**Groudon** (Japanese: **グラードン** _Groudon_) is a [recurring Pokémon character](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_recurring_wild_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_the_animated_series 'List of recurring wild Pokémon in the animated series') in [_Pokémon the Series: XY_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY 'Pokémon the Series: XY') .

### History

Groudon made a cameo appearance in _[Mega Evolution Special II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS02 'XYS02') _, where it was shown being awakened by the presence of the [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') .

It played a bigger role in _[Mega Evolution Special III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS03 'XYS03') _, where it was shown in its [primal state](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') . As it made its way to the Giant Rock it left a trail of boiling magma behind it, making the area incredibly warm. It fought with Primal Kyogre over the Giant Rock and defeated [Alain's Charizard](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain%27s_Charizard "Alain's Charizard") . Primal Groudon was quickly defeated by Mega Rayquaza's [Dragon Ascent](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dragon_Ascent_(move)> 'Dragon Ascent (move)') and reverted to its normal form.

The same Groudon reappeared in flashbacks during _[Cloudy Fate, Bright Future!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XY092 'XY092') _ and _[The First Day of the Rest of Your Life!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XY138 'XY138') _.

### Personality and characteristics

Primal Groudon was fixated on the [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') and was prepared to attack anyone who interfered with its plans. Not much was seen of Groudon's personality as it was mostly seen in a fit of rage.

**Rayquaza** (Japanese: **レックウザ** _Rayquaza_) is a [recurring Pokémon character](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_recurring_wild_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_the_animated_series 'List of recurring wild Pokémon in the animated series') in [_Pokémon the Series: XY_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY 'Pokémon the Series: XY') .

### History

[Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') first appeared in _[Mega Evolution Special II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS02 'XYS02') _, where its slumber was disturbed by [Alain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain 'Alain') and the gang when they excavated a giant [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') . It appears in a fit of rage and violently attacked, fighting against Alain and [Steven](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven_Stone 'Steven Stone') with their [Mega Charizard X](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain%27s_Charizard "Alain's Charizard") and [Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Shiny Pokémon') [Mega Metagross](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven%27s_Metagross "Steven's Metagross") , gaining the upper hand before destroying the megalith with [Draco Meteor](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Draco_Meteor_(move)> 'Draco Meteor (move)') . It then left and returned to the ozone layer.

It reappeared in _[Mega Evolution Special III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS03 'XYS03') _, where it stopped a battle between Primal [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') and Primal [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') .

### Personality and characteristics

Not much was seen of Rayquaza's personality, as it was mostly seen in a fit of rage. However, it was shown to stop the violent clash between Primal Kyogre and Primal Groudon when the two clashed.
