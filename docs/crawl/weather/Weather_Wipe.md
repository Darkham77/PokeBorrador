**Weather Wipe** (Japanese: **Ｐ技後天気解除** _Remove Weather After Pokémon Uses Move_) is a family of [skills](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Skill_(Masters)> 'Skill (Masters)') in [Pokémon Masters EX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Masters_EX 'Pokémon Masters EX') .

Contents

## Effect

When the [sync pair](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_pair 'Sync pair') 's Pokémon uses a move, it has a chance of removing the any [weather](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Masters)> 'Weather (Masters)') condition currently applied to the field of play. The skill can range from Weather Wipe 1 to Weather Wipe 9, with higher numbered skills resulting in a greater chance of removing weather conditions. Weather Wipe 9 will always remove any weather condition. Currently, only Weather Wipe 9 is available on scoutable [sync pairs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sync_pair 'Sync pair') .

| Weather Wipe 5 | 60%               |
