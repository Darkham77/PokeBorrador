The **weather** (Japanese: **天気** _weather_) is a set of mechanics in the [Pokémon games](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_games "Pokémon games")
.

Contents

In the core series games
------------------------

In the [core series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Core_series "Core series")
, the weather changes the battle environment, activating [Abilities](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ability "Ability")
, modifying certain [moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Move "Move")
, and potentially damaging the [Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)")
 in battle or affecting their [stats](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stats "Stats")
. They have been included in every [core series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Core_series "Core series")
 game since [Generation II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_II "Generation II")
. Only one type of weather may be present at a time; an effect that creates weather will remove the previous weather.

In Generation II, the only way to cause weather was by a Pokémon using a [move](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Move "Move")
 in-battle. [Generation III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_III "Generation III")
 introduced [weather-related Abilities](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Category:Abilities_with_effects_on_weather_conditions "Category:Abilities with effects on weather conditions")
 as well as overworld weather; starting a battle in an overworld location with weather would cause that weather to be automatically present from the beginning of the battle. [Generation IV](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_IV "Generation IV")
 introduced four held items ([Heat](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Heat_Rock "Heat Rock")
, [Damp](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Damp_Rock "Damp Rock")
, [Smooth](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Smooth_Rock "Smooth Rock")
, and [Icy Rocks](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Icy_Rock "Icy Rock")
) that increase the duration of weather caused by [weather-changing moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Category:Weather-changing_moves "Category:Weather-changing moves")
 or [Abilities](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ability "Ability")
. [Generation V](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_V "Generation V")
 introduced [seasons](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Season_(game_mechanic) "Season (game mechanic)")
, which may affect the weather in certain locations, and [Generation VI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_VI "Generation VI")
 introduced a new [Evolution method](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Methods_of_Evolution "Methods of Evolution")
 affected by the weather. [Generation VIII](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_VIII "Generation VIII")
 introduced overworld effects that resemble weather but do not directly correspond with an in-battle weather.

#### Moves affected by weather

Certain specific moves are also affected in special ways by the weather.

* The recovery moves [Synthesis](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Synthesis_(move) "Synthesis (move)")
    , [Morning Sun](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Morning_Sun_(move) "Morning Sun (move)")
    , and [Moonlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Moonlight_(move) "Moonlight (move)")
     restore more HP than usual in harsh sunlight, and less than usual in most other weather.
  * In Generation II, during no weather they normally restore ¼ total HP, during [harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight "Harsh sunlight")
         they normally restore ½ total HP, and during other weather they normally restore ⅛ total HP. Except in link battles, the amount of HP restored is doubled if it is the move's preferred time of day (morning for Morning Sun, day for Synthesis, night for Moonlight).
  * From Generation III onward, during no weather or [strong winds](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Strong_winds "Strong winds")
         they restore ½ total HP, during [harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight "Harsh sunlight")
         they restore ⅔ total HP, and during other weather they restore ¼ total HP.
* The move [Shore Up](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shore_Up_(move) "Shore Up (move)")
     restores ⅔ of the user's total HP during a sandstorm; outside of a sandstorm, it only restores ½ total HP.
* The accuracy of [Thunder](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Thunder_(move) "Thunder (move)")
    , [Hurricane](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hurricane_(move) "Hurricane (move)")
    , [Blizzard](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blizzard_(move) "Blizzard (move)")
    , [Bleakwind Storm](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bleakwind_Storm_(move) "Bleakwind Storm (move)")
    , [Wildbolt Storm](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wildbolt_Storm_(move) "Wildbolt Storm (move)")
    , and [Sandsear Storm](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sandsear_Storm_(move) "Sandsear Storm (move)")
     are affected by the weather.
  * From [Generation IV](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_IV "Generation IV")
         onward, during hail Blizzard ignores accuracy and evasion checks.
  * During rain, Thunder and Hurricane ignore accuracy and evasion checks; during harsh sunlight, Thunder and Hurricane only have 50% accuracy.
  * In Generation IX, during rain, Bleakwind Storm, Wildbolt Storm, and Sandsear Storm ignore accuracy and evasion checks.
  * In [Pokémon Diamond and Pearl](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Diamond_and_Pearl_Versions "Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Versions")
         only, Blizzard and Thunder also have a 30% chance to hit through [Protect](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Protect_(move) "Protect (move)")
         or [Detect](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Detect_(move) "Detect (move)")
         when used in hail or rain, respectively.
* [Solar Beam](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Solar_Beam_(move) "Solar Beam (move)")
     and [Solar Blade](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Solar_Blade_(move) "Solar Blade (move)")
     become one-turn moves in harsh sunlight, skipping their charge turn; in rain, sandstorm, hail, and fog, the moves' powers are halved.
* [Electro Shot](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Electro_Shot_(move) "Electro Shot (move)")
     becomes a one-turn move in rain, first raising the user's [Special Attack](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stat#Special_Attack "Stat")
     and then attacking in the same turn.
* From Generation V onward, during harsh sunlight [Growth](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Growth_(move) "Growth (move)")
     increases the user's [Attack](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stat#Attack "Stat")
     and [Special Attack](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stat#Special_Attack "Stat")
     by two stages each instead of one.
* In Generations IV and V, and [Pokémon X and Y](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_X_and_Y "Pokémon X and Y")
    , when used as a [field move](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Field_move "Field move")
     [Sweet Scent](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sweet_Scent_(move) "Sweet Scent (move)")
     fails if there is a non-aesthetic weather effect in the area.
* The move [Aurora Veil](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aurora_Veil_(move) "Aurora Veil (move)")
     can only be used successfully during hail or snow; otherwise it fails.

### Overworld

There are a large variety of weather conditions that can occur in the overworld, only some of which have an effect in battle. If a weather condition is present in an area, the corresponding weather effect will appear in every battle in that location; this weather condition remains in effect permanently, unless it is replaced by another weather condition (due to a move, Ability, etc.). In some games, if the natural weather condition is replaced in battle, when that effect ends, the original weather condition will return.

In [Sinnoh](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sinnoh "Sinnoh")
, the move [Defog](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Defog_(move) "Defog (move)")
 can remove the [fog](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fog "Fog")
 weather condition from the overworld when used as a [field move](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Field_move "Field move")
.

[Diamond Dust](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Easter_Egg#Diamond_Dust "Easter Egg")
 is a special, purely aesthetic version of light snow, and replaces hail where applicable, allowing the player to battle without the effects of hail, without changing the weather manually. It first appeared as an [Easter egg](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Easter_egg "Easter egg")
 in [Pokémon Diamond and Pearl](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Diamond_and_Pearl_Versions "Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Versions")
, and has appeared in several [core series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Core_series "Core series")
 games since.

In the spin-off games
---------------------

### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

_Main article: [Weather (Mystery Dungeon)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Mystery_Dungeon) "Weather (Mystery Dungeon)")
_

Various weather effects appear in certain dungeons in the [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon_series "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series")
. Weather effects can change from floor to floor, and can also be altered through the use of [Wonder Orbs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wonder_Orb "Wonder Orb")
. In [Gates to Infinity](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
, Pokémon will regenerate HP only if the weather is Clear, or if it is holding a [Weather Band](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Scarf_(Mystery_Dungeon)#Weather_Band "Scarf (Mystery Dungeon)")
. In [Super Mystery Dungeon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
 and [Rescue Team DX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
, Pokémon can regenerate HP in any weather as long as they do not take damage from the weather condition, or if it is holding a [Weather Looplet](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Looplet#Weather_Looplet "Looplet")
.

### Pokémon GO

_Main article: [Weather (GO)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(GO) "Weather (GO)")
_

Various weather effects appear in [Pokémon GO](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_GO "Pokémon GO")
, intended to reflect the actual weather. Weather in each local area is updated every hour, on the hour, via [Accuweather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accuweather "wp:Accuweather")
. Each weather offers various weather boosts to Pokémon belonging to specific types.

### Pokémon Masters EX

_Main article: [Weather (Masters)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_(Masters) "Weather (Masters)")
_

Weather functions similarly in [Pokémon Masters EX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Masters_EX "Pokémon Masters EX")
 as it does in the main series games, though some of the effects are slightly tweaked.

* Sunny: Boosts Fire-type attacks by 50%. Additionally, Pokémon cannot be frozen, and previous Pokémon that were frozen prior to the weather condition being implemented will not be frozen anymore after the weather takes effect.
* Rain: Boosts Water-type attacks by 50%.
* Hail: Damages Pokémon after every action.
* Sandstorm: Damages Pokémon after every action.

    .
