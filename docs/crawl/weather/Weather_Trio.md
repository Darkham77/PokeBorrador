## Shared design

The three super-ancient Pokémon all have some physiological similarities, with similar symbols on their bodies as well as yellow eyes.

### Abilities

Groudon, Kyogre, and Rayquaza all have [weather-changing](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Category:Abilities_with_effects_on_weather_conditions 'Category:Abilities with effects on weather conditions') [Abilities](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ability 'Ability') . In their debut appearance in the Generation III games, their Abilities were exclusive to them (although [Cloud Nine](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cloud_Nine_(Ability)> 'Cloud Nine (Ability)') has the same effect as Rayquaza's [Air Lock](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Air_Lock_(Ability)> 'Air Lock (Ability)') Ability). Upon undergoing Primal Reversion or Mega Evolution, Groudon, Kyogre, and Rayquaza gain new exclusive Abilities, which are enhanced versions of their original Abilities.

Groudon and Kyogre have [Drought](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Drought_(Ability)> 'Drought (Ability)') and [Drizzle](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Drizzle_(Ability)> 'Drizzle (Ability)') , which create [harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Harsh_sunlight 'Harsh sunlight') and [rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain 'Rain') , respectively. From Generation III to V, these Abilities created weather that lasted for the remainder of the battle—this contrasts with the moves [Sunny Day](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sunny_Day_(move)> 'Sunny Day (move)') and [Rain Dance](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rain_Dance_(move)> 'Rain Dance (move)') , which only create weather for 5 turns. This was changed in Generation VI, so that these Abilities also only create weather for 5 turns.

Rayquaza's Air Lock Ability removes the effects of any weather as long as Rayquaza remains in battle, allowing it to remove the effects of the Abilities of Groudon and Kyogre.

Primal Groudon, Primal Kyogre, and Mega Rayquaza have the Abilities [Desolate Land](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Desolate_Land_(Ability)> 'Desolate Land (Ability)') , [Primordial Sea](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primordial_Sea_(Ability)> 'Primordial Sea (Ability)') , and [Delta Stream](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Delta_Stream_(Ability)> 'Delta Stream (Ability)') , which create the special weather conditions [extremely harsh sunlight](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Extremely_harsh_sunlight 'Extremely harsh sunlight') , [heavy rain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Heavy_rain 'Heavy rain') , and [strong winds](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Strong_winds 'Strong winds') , respectively. These weather conditions last as long as they remain in battle. Extremely harsh sunlight and heavy rain are enhanced versions of harsh sunlight and rain, having all the same effects as the base weather, but providing additional benefits and being harder to remove. All three weather conditions cannot be replaced by other weather conditions while a Pokémon with the corresponding Ability remains on the field, except each other.

### Origin

The three Pokémon represent separate elemental spheres: Kyogre having dominion over the [hydrosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/hydrosphere 'wp:hydrosphere') , Groudon over the [lithosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/lithosphere 'wp:lithosphere') , and Rayquaza over the [atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/atmosphere 'wp:atmosphere') . These roles are reflected in their types. The three Pokémon could be references to creatures from Hebrew myth: [Leviathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leviathan 'wp:Leviathan') , a sea monster; [Behemoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behemoth 'wp:Behemoth') , a land beast; and [Ziz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziz 'wp:Ziz') , a giant bird.

### Core series games

Thousands of years ago, [Primal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') and Primal [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') were formed by the natural energy that existed in that time and then proceed to fill the sea and create land, respectively. Meanwhile, [Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') resided in the ozone layer, where it resided in and fed on water, other particles, and [meteorites](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Meteorite 'Meteorite') . At some point later, a chance meeting turned Kyogre and Groudon into rivals, who then began a cataclysmic conflict for the dominance of the [Pokémon world](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_world 'Pokémon world') . This battle continues until Rayquaza eventually descends from the skies and quells it, forcing both of them to retreat and be sealed into underground caverns: Kyogre in [Seafloor Cavern](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seafloor_Cavern 'Seafloor Cavern') and Groudon in Seafloor Cavern**[RS](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Ruby_and_Sapphire_Versions 'Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire Versions') \*\***[ORAS](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Omega_Ruby_and_Alpha_Sapphire 'Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire') **/under [Mt. Chimney](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mt._Chimney 'Mt. Chimney') **[E](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Emerald_Version 'Pokémon Emerald Version') \*\*, where they slumbered in since then.[\[5\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_trio#cite_note-5)

2,000 years ago, in a time known as the primal age, the world was overflowing with natural energy, so Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre reawakened to fight for more of that energy in long clashes,[\[6\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_trio#cite_note-6) creating natural disasters in the process. The [Hoenn](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn 'Hoenn') [region](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Region 'Region') was created from these same clashes. In the face of their great power, people could do nothing, but to their relief, Rayquaza intervened once again. Its power overwhelmed that of the two Primal Pokémon, ending their conflict, putting both to sleep once again, and returning peace to the world.

1,000 years ago, a meteoroid hit the earth, creating the crater in which [Sootopolis City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sootopolis_City 'Sootopolis City') would be built in. This impact caused cracks in the ground that unleashed natural energy, causing Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre to awaken and battle each other once again for 20 days, threatening to destroy the world in the process.[\[7\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather_trio#cite_note-7) The [Draconid people](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Draconid_people 'Draconid people') had a collective memory of how Rayquaza saved their race long ago, and their wish for this to happen again combined with the power of a [meteorite](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Key_Stone 'Key Stone') allowed Rayquaza to [Mega Evolve](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution 'Mega Evolution') for the first time and calm down the two Primal Pokémon once more. Stripped of their primal forms, Groudon and Kyogre entered a deep slumber in underground caverns while Rayquaza returned to the sky.

At some point, [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') was then created by the Draconids to honor of Rayquaza and to preserve the history of the three super-ancient Pokémon. They also prepared for the day when Rayquaza would be needed again in 1,000, when [another meteoroid](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Grand_Meteor_Delta 'Grand Meteor Delta') would appear on a collision course with Hoenn. The [Red Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Red_Orb 'Red Orb') and [Blue Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blue_Orb 'Blue Orb') , were also created to control Groudon and Kyogre in case they reawakened.

## In the core series games

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/a/aa/Super-ancient_Pok%C3%A9mon_cutscene.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Super-ancient_Pok%C3%A9mon_cutscene.png)

Rayquaza confronting Kyogre and Groudon in a cutscene in Emerald.

### Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire

In these games, both Kyogre and Groudon appear in the [Cave of Origin](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cave_of_Origin 'Cave of Origin') in their respective games, and after entering the [Hall of Fame](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hall_of_Fame 'Hall of Fame') , the [player](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Player_character 'Player character') can encounter Rayquaza at rest on the topmost floor of the [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') .

### Pokémon Emerald

In the enhanced version of Ruby and Sapphire, Kyogre and Groudon both appear in alternating caves after entering the Hall of Fame: [Marine Cave](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Marine_Cave 'Marine Cave') (on [Route 105](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_105 'Hoenn Route 105') , [Route 125](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_125 'Hoenn Route 125') , [Route 127](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_127 'Hoenn Route 127') , or [Route 129](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_129 'Hoenn Route 129') ) for Kyogre and [Terra Cave](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Terra_Cave 'Terra Cave') (on [Route 114](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_114 'Hoenn Route 114') , [Route 115](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_115 'Hoenn Route 115') , [Route 116](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_116 'Hoenn Route 116') , or [Route 118](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_118 'Hoenn Route 118') ) for Groudon. Rayquaza appears atop the [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') after calming Kyogre and Groudon in [Sootopolis City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sootopolis_City 'Sootopolis City') , and is obtainable before entering the Hall of Fame.

### Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver

All three super-ancient Pokémon can be obtained in the [Embedded Tower](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Embedded_Tower 'Embedded Tower') after [Red](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Red_(game)> 'Red (game)') is defeated and [player](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Player_character 'Player character') has the [National Pokédex](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/National_Pok%C3%A9dex 'National Pokédex') , [Kanto first partner Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_first_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Kanto first partner Pokémon') , and respective [Orbs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colored_orbs 'Colored orbs') . Kyogre is obtainable in HeartGold, and Groudon is obtainable in SoulSilver. Once both a Kyogre from HeartGold and a Groudon from SoulSilver are in one game, the player gets an opportunity to catch Rayquaza.

### Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire

In the [remakes](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Remake 'Remake') , both Kyogre and Groudon appear in the [Cave of Origin](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cave_of_Origin 'Cave of Origin') just like in the originals. However, when the player faces them, they are in their [Primal Forms](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') . Rayquaza is obtained during the Delta Episode atop [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') , which occurs after the player become the Champion and is granted access to Sky Pillar by [Wallace](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wallace 'Wallace') . Rayquaza must be caught when encountered; if the player runs away from it or defeats it, they will have to battle it again until it is caught.

### Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon

All three members of the super-ancient Pokémon can be encountered in the [Ultra Space Wilds](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ultra_Space_Wilds 'Ultra Space Wilds') through [Ultra Warp Ride](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ultra_Warp_Ride 'Ultra Warp Ride') . [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') **[US](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Ultra_Sun_and_Ultra_Moon 'Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon') ** can be encountered in the Cave World through a yellow warp hole. [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') **[UM](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Ultra_Sun_and_Ultra_Moon 'Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon') ** can be encountered in the Water World through a blue warp hole. [Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') can be encountered in the Cliff World through a red warp hole, as long as both Groudon and Kyogre are in the player's party.

### Pokémon Sword and Shield

In [The Crown Tundra](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/The_Crown_Tundra 'The Crown Tundra') [DLC](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Downloadable_content 'Downloadable content') , the super-ancient Pokémon be found at [Max Lair](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Max_Lair 'Max Lair') during [Dynamax Adventures](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dynamax_Adventure 'Dynamax Adventure') . [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') can be found in Sword, while [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') can be found in Shield; however, the player can get the other Legendary by participating in other players' Dynamax Adventures.

### Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl

The super-ancient Pokémon can all be encountered at [Ramanas Park](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ramanas_Park 'Ramanas Park') after capturing the [legendary giants](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_giants 'Legendary giants') , [Legendary beasts](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_beasts 'Legendary beasts') **[BD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Brilliant_Diamond_and_Shining_Pearl 'Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl') **/[Legendary birds](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_birds 'Legendary birds') **[SP](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Brilliant_Diamond_and_Shining_Pearl 'Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl') ** and [Eon duo](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Eon_duo 'Eon duo') . The trio is located at Oceanic Room, Tectonic Room, and Stratospheric Room respectively. Each room needs either Oceanic Slate, Tectonic Slate, or Stratospheric Slate to unlock the encounter.

### Pokémon Scarlet and Violet

A [Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Shiny Pokémon') Rayquaza was made available in a limited [Poké Portal News](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Portal_News 'Poké Portal News') event called **Shiny Rayquaza Appears**. This event lasted from December 19, 2024 to January 5, 2025. During the [Tera Raid](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tera_Raid 'Tera Raid') Battle event, the Shiny Rayquaza can be caught once per save file.

#### [The Indigo Disk](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/The_Indigo_Disk 'The Indigo Disk')

In the DLC, the super-ancient Pokémon can be obtained via [treats](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Treat 'Treat') from [Snacksworth](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Snacksworth 'Snacksworth') . Groudon can be found in Scarlet via the [Groudon Treat](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_Treat 'Groudon Treat') , while Kyogre can be found in Violet via the [Kyogre Treat](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_Treat 'Kyogre Treat') . The player can get the other Legendary by participating in [Blueberry Quests](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blueberry_Quest 'Blueberry Quest') through the [Union Circle](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Union_Circle 'Union Circle') online. Rayquaza can be obtained through the exclusive online group quests via the [Rayquaza Treat](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_Treat 'Rayquaza Treat') .

### Pokémon Legends: Z-A

In the [Mega Dimension](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Dimension 'Mega Dimension') DLC, the super-ancient Pokémon can be obtained in [Hyperspace Lumiose](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hyperspace_Lumiose 'Hyperspace Lumiose') . Groudon can be found in [Hyperspace Desolate Land](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hyperspace_Desolate_Land 'Hyperspace Desolate Land') , being accessed by [Ansha](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ansha 'Ansha') 's [Hoopa](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ansha#Pok%C3%A9mon 'Ansha') via the [Omega Old-Fashioned Donut](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Donut#Donut_types 'Donut') . Kyogre can be found in [Hyperspace Primordial Sea](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hyperspace_Primordial_Sea 'Hyperspace Primordial Sea') , being accessed by Ansha's Hoopa via the [Alpha Old-Fashioned Donut](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Donut#Donut_types 'Donut') . Rayquaza is caught for Ansha, who dreams of meeting it so she can gift it to her [mother](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Diantha 'Diantha') .

[![201](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/c/c4/Ani201ODEX.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Ani201ODEX.png '201')

In [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team') , as well as their [remake](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Remake 'Remake') , [Rescue Team DX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX') , Groudon and Rayquaza serve notable roles in the story. Groudon awakens after the player has visited [Mt. Freeze](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mt._Freeze 'Mt. Freeze') and spoken with [Ninetales](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ninetales_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Ninetales (Pokémon)') . After [Team A.C.T.](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_A.C.T. 'Team A.C.T.') and another team have failed to defeat it, the player's team must travel to [Magma Cavern](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magma_Cavern 'Magma Cavern') to stop it. Rayquaza, meanwhile, serves as the final boss of the main game. The player and their partner must climb to the top of the [Sky Tower](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Tower 'Sky Tower') in order to ask Rayquaza's help in destroying a giant meteor that's threatening the planet. While Rayquaza initially attacks them due to them intruding on his territory, it sees reason at the last second and uses [Hyper Beam](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hyper_Beam_(move)> 'Hyper Beam (move)') to destroy the meteor just before it can hit.

During the post-game, each of the super-ancient Pokémon can be recruited by defeating them on the final floor of a dungeon. Kyogre resides at the bottom of the [Stormy Sea](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stormy_Sea 'Stormy Sea') , while Groudon and Rayquaza can be found in the same dungeons as during the main game. In the remake, Kyogre and Groudon will undergo [Primal Reversion](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') while being fought, while Rayquaza will [Mega Evolve](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution 'Mega Evolution') .

In [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness') and [Explorers of Sky](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky 'Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky') , the player and partner encounter a Groudon at the end of [Steam Cave](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steam_Cave 'Steam Cave') , though after it is defeated, it turns out to have been just an illusion created by [Uxie](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Uxie_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Uxie (Pokémon)') to protect the [Time Gear](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Time_Gear 'Time Gear') of [Fogbound Lake](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fogbound_Lake 'Fogbound Lake') . During the post-game, each of the super-ancient Pokémon can be found in a dungeon, protecting one of the [Seven Treasures](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seven_Treasures 'Seven Treasures') and needing to be defeated in order to obtain them.

In [Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon 'Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon') , Rayquaza makes several brief appearances during the main game, fleeing the planet in fear of the [Dark Matter](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dark_Matter 'Dark Matter') , though it eventually returns to prevent the Dark Matter from taking the [Tree of Life](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tree_of_Life 'Tree of Life') away from the planet, teaming up with [Deoxys](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Deoxys_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Deoxys (Pokémon)') to do so. Though the Dark Matter turns both of them to stone soon after, they're restored to normal following Dark Matter's defeat. During the post-game, each of the super-ancient Pokémon serves as the boss of a dungeon, appearing in their alternate forms.

### _Pokémon the Series_

All three super-ancient Pokémon have appeared in [_Pokémon the Series_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series 'Pokémon the Series') .

#### \_[Destiny Deoxys](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/M07 'M07')

\_

_Main article: [Rayquaza (M07)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(M07) "Rayquaza (M07)") \_

Rayquaza first appeared in _[Destiny Deoxys](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/M07 'M07') _, where it defended its territory from a [Deoxys](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Deoxys_(M07)> 'Deoxys (M07)') .

Primal Kyogre, Primal Groudon, and a [Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Shiny Pokémon') Mega Rayquaza all made a major appearance in _[Hoopa and The Clash of Ages](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/M18 'M18') _. Primal Kyogre and Primal Groudon were summoned by a dark entity of [Hoopa](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoopa_(anime)> 'Hoopa (anime)') 's [Unbound](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_with_form_differences#Hoopa 'List of Pokémon with form differences') form, along with the [Pokémon of myth](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_of_myth 'Pokémon of myth') and [Kyurem](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyurem_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyurem (Pokémon)') , and were controlled by the evil shadow in order to terrorize [Dahara City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dahara_City 'Dahara City') . It was revealed that long ago, Kyogre and Groudon engaged in a battle with Hoopa in its Unbound form to entertain the citizens of Dahara City, but this ended when Hoopa's power was sealed by [Ghris](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ghris 'Ghris') . Mega Rayquaza was summoned by Hoopa along with [Latias](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Latias_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Latias (Pokémon)') and [Latios](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Latios_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Latios (Pokémon)') in order to stop the entity of Hoopa's Unbound form from terrorizing Dahara City.

#### [_Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_Ruby_and_Sapphire 'Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire')

_Main article: [Kyogre and Groudon (Ruby and Sapphire series)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_and_Groudon_(Ruby*and_Sapphire_series) "Kyogre and Groudon (Ruby and Sapphire series)") *

Kyogre and Groudon both debuted in _[Gaining Groudon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/AG097 'AG097') _. Kyogre had been captured by [Team Magma](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Magma 'Team Magma') , who was also in possession of the [Blue Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blue_Orb 'Blue Orb') . Groudon was in [Team Aqua](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Aqua 'Team Aqua') 's possession along with the [Red Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Red_Orb 'Red Orb') . The two teams were planning on swapping Groudon and Kyogre, but Kyogre was released by [Team Aqua commander](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aqua_Admin_(Trainer_class)> 'Aqua Admin (Trainer class)') [Shelly](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shelly 'Shelly') , who had infiltrated Team Magma. [Archie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Archie 'Archie') then proceeded to take control of Kyogre using the Red Orb. In the [following episode](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/AG098 'AG098') , Archie threatened to use Kyogre's power to flood the island both teams had met at. At this time the now awakened Groudon came and began to battle with Kyogre. During this time, [Ash](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ash_Ketchum 'Ash Ketchum') , [his friends](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ash%27s_friends "Ash's friends") , and [Lance](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lance_(anime)> 'Lance (anime)') managed to snap Archie out of the Red Orb's possession. Now free from the Orbs' possession Kyogre and Groudon retreated into the ocean and a volcano, respectively.

Rayquaza in [_Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_Ruby_and_Sapphire 'Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire')

#### [Mega Evolution Specials](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution_Specials 'Mega Evolution Specials')

_Main article: [Super-ancient Pokémon (XY series)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Super-ancient_Pok%C3%A9mon_(XY*series) "Super-ancient Pokémon (XY series)") *

Kyogre and Groudon make prominent appearances in the [Mega Evolution Specials](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution_Specials 'Mega Evolution Specials') of [_Pokémon the Series: XY_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY 'Pokémon the Series: XY') .

In _[Mega Evolution Special II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS02 'XYS02') _, Rayquaza appeared after [Alain](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain 'Alain') and [Steven Stone](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven_Stone_(anime)> 'Steven Stone (anime)') used their [Mega Ring](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Ring 'Mega Ring') and [Mega Stickpin](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Key_Stone 'Key Stone') , respectively, to open one of Hoenn's ancient ruins and reveal the legendary [Giant Rock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giant_Rock 'Giant Rock') . The energy emitted by the rock not only brought Rayquaza to the ruins, but it also awoke Groudon and Kyogre from their slumbers. Rayquaza, in a fit of rage, began attacking the people responsible for unlocking the Giant Rock. Seeing that, Alain and Steven sent out [Charizard](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alain%27s_Charizard "Alain's Charizard") and [Metagross](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven%27s_Metagross "Steven's Metagross") to stop the [Sky High Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_category 'Pokémon category') , but to no avail. Rayquaza destroyed the ruins by using [Draco Meteor](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Draco_Meteor_(move)> 'Draco Meteor (move)') , disintegrating the Giant Rock in the process before returning to the ozone layer.

In _[Mega Evolution Special III](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/XYS03 'XYS03') _, Alain and Steven were monitoring the area around [Rustboro City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rustboro_City 'Rustboro City') when they noticed that Groudon had awakened and was seemingly moving towards the coastline. Steven wondered if Kyogre could also appear, and just at that moment, they received footage of the [Sea Basin Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_category 'Pokémon category') . Lysandre then commented on the possibility of the [Pokémon world](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_world 'Pokémon world') being destroyed, scaring Mairin. After that, Steven explained to her that there is a legend in the [Hoenn](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn 'Hoenn') [region](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Region 'Region') that has been told since ancient times. According to Steven, the legend says that in the prehistoric era, Primal Groudon expanded the continents and Primal Kyogre expanded the seas. The two [Legendary Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Legendary Pokémon') were locked in a fierce struggle to obtain a source of enormous energy. That struggle shook the land and the skies, nearly destroying the world in the process. He also added that some people believe the energy source they were searching for was the Giant Rock.

Steven and Lysandre soon discovered that both Groudon and Kyogre were following an energy similar to that of the Giant Rock. Suspecting the rock would materialize in the place Groudon and Kyogre were headed, Lysandre ordered Alain to protect it from the super-ancient Pokémon. After that, Alain and Steven flew over the area on a plane and, when the two [Primal Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') started fighting for the source of energy, they sent out their Charizard and Metagross to prevent them from consuming the rock's energy. However, Charizard and Metagross had trouble fighting against the Legendary Pokémon, but their combined efforts protected the Giant Rock long enough for Rayquaza to step in and put an end to the conflict between Groudon and Kyogre, causing them to return to their regular forms. After that, [Lysandre's employees](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Flare 'Team Flare') appeared to take away the Giant Rock, blocking its energy and placing it in a submarine. With the rock away, all three super-ancient Pokémon retreated.

[Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Shiny Pokémon') Rayquaza in [_Pokémon Horizons: The Series_](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Horizons:_The_Series 'Pokémon Horizons: The Series')

### _Pokémon Horizons: The Series_

_Main article: [Black Rayquaza](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Black_Rayquaza 'Black Rayquaza') _

A Shiny Rayquaza known as the Black Rayquaza plays a major role in the series. It was once owned by the Ancient Adventurer [Lucius](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lucius 'Lucius') , contained in an [Ancient Poké Ball](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball#Ancient_Pok%C3%A9_Ball 'Poké Ball') that eventually wound up in [Roy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Roy 'Roy') 's possession. He initially thought the Poké Ball was empty, until _[The Ancient Poké Ball](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/HZ006 'HZ006') _, where it reacted with [Liko](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Liko 'Liko') 's pendant and opened to reveal Rayquaza, which subsequently flew away, setting up Roy's dream to follow and find it. Roy and [Ult](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ult 'Ult') battled it in _[Destiny of the Black Rayquaza](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/HZ129 'HZ129') _ and won, resulting in it allowing Roy to catch it. It then flew away to reunite the [Six Heroes](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Six_Heroes 'Six Heroes') .

Kyogre and Groudon expended all of their energy in stopping just a fragment of the meteoroid, leaving it up to Rayquaza and the [humans](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Human 'Human') of the world to stop the main body of the meteoroid.

- The term "super-ancient Pokémon" has previously been attributed to [Fossil Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fossil 'Fossil') in the Japanese version of the [animated series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_animated_series 'Pokémon animated series') .
- The [colors](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_by_color 'List of Pokémon by color') of the three super-ancient Pokémon are the [additive primary colors of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RGB_color_model 'wp:RGB color model') .
  - All three super-ancient Pokémon (while transformed) have [Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Shiny Pokémon') variations that are black. However, only Shiny [Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') is black in its normal state, as a Shiny [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') is purple while a Shiny [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') is yellow.
- The super-ancient Pokémon are the only [Legendary trio](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_trio 'Legendary trio') in which all of its members live in different [habitats](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_by_habitat 'List of Pokémon by habitat') — Kyogre lives in the Sea habitat, Groudon lives in the Rough-Terrain habitat, and Rayquaza lives in the Rare Habitat.
