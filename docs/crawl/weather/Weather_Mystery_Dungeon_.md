There are several **[weather](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Weather "Weather")
** effects in the [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon_series "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series")
.

The weather can change from floor to floor in the [dungeons](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mystery_dungeon "Mystery dungeon")
, and can also be altered through the use of [Wonder Orbs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wonder_Orb "Wonder Orb")
.

Effects
-------

* **Clear:** No effect**[RB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team")
    ****[TD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness")
    [S](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky")
    ****[BSL](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon_(WiiWare) "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon (WiiWare)")
    **. Pokémon will restore [HP](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/HP "HP")
     naturally as turns go by**[GtI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
    ****[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
    ****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
    **.
* **Sunny:** Fire-type moves increase in power by 50%, while the power of Water-type moves is cut in half, or reduced by 1/4 starting in Gates to Infinity. Pokémon cannot be [frozen](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Freeze_(status_condition) "Freeze (status condition)")
    **[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
    ****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
    **. Also turns [Castform](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Castform_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Castform (Pokémon)")
     into a Fire-type.
* **Fog:** The power of Electric-type moves is cut in half. Also turns Castform into a Water-type in [Red and Blue Rescue Team](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red and Blue Rescue Team")
    . This weather effect is not present since Gates to Infinity.
* **Snow:** Ice-type Pokémon have an increased [Movement Speed](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Travel_Speed "Travel Speed")
    . Also turns Castform into an Ice-type. This weather effect is not present since Gates to Infinity.
* **Rain:** Water-type moves increase in power by 50%, while the power of Fire-type moves is cut in half, or reduced by 1/4 starting in Gates to Infinity. The floor becomes defused, preventing explosions from traps and moves like [Self-Destruct](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Self-Destruct_(move) "Self-Destruct (move)")
    . Also turns Castform into a Water-type.
* **Hail:** Deals 5**[RB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team")
    **/3**[TD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness")
    [S](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky")
    ****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
    **/1**[GtI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
    ****[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
    ** points of damage to non-Ice types every 10 turns. Also turns Castform into an Ice-type.
* **Sandstorm:** Deals 5**[RB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team")
    **/3**[TD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness")
    [S](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky")
    ****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
    **/1**[GtI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
    ****[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
    ** points of damage to non-Rock, Ground, and Steel types every 10 turns. Rock-type Pokémon take less damage from special attacks (except in Red and Blue Rescue Team).
* **Cloudy** (Japanese: **くもり** _Cloudy_): Reduces the damage of all non-Normal-type moves by 25%. This weather effect is not present since Gates to Infinity.
* **Extremely Harsh Sunlight:** Fire-type attacking moves increase power by 50%, while Water-type attacking moves are nullified. This weather effect is only available in Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon and Rescue Team DX.
* **Heavy Rain:** Water-type moves increase power by 50%, while Fire-type moves are nullified. The floor becomes defused. This weather effect is only available in Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon and Rescue Team DX.
* **Strong Winds:** Moves that would be super effective against pure Flying-type Pokémon will only be normally effective. This weather effect is only available in Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon and Rescue Team DX.
* **Magma** (Japanese: **マグマ** _Magma_): All Pokémon are moved when the ground shakes. All Pokémon except for [Fire-type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fire_(type) "Fire (type)")
     Pokémon that touch the magma will get a [Burn](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition_(Mystery_Dungeon)#Burn "Status condition (Mystery Dungeon)")
    . The magma can be removed with [Primordial Sea](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primordial_Sea_(Ability) "Primordial Sea (Ability)")
    . This weather effect is only available in Rescue Team DX and is exclusive to the [Groudon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Groudon (Pokémon)")
     boss fight in [Magma Cavern](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magma_Cavern "Magma Cavern")
    .
* **Gust** (Japanese: **とっぷう** _Gust_): Pokémon hit by a gust will be moved and may be damaged. This weather effect is only available in Rescue Team DX and is exclusive to the [Rayquaza](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Rayquaza (Pokémon)")
     boss fight at the peak of [Sky Tower](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Tower "Sky Tower")
    .
* **Flooded** (Japanese: **しんすい** _Flooded_): All Pokémon are washed away when the water floods the dungeon. All Pokémon except for [Water-type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Water_(type) "Water (type)")
     Pokémon will also become [Slow](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition_(Mystery_Dungeon)#Slow "Status condition (Mystery Dungeon)")
    . The water can be removed with [Desolate Land](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Desolate_Land_(Ability) "Desolate Land (Ability)")
    . This weather effect is only available in Rescue Team DX and is exclusive to the [Kyogre](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Kyogre (Pokémon)")
     boss fight in [Stormy Sea](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stormy_Sea "Stormy Sea")
    .
* **Rainbow Light** (Japanese: **にじのひかり** _Rainbow Light_): Periodically restores 50 HP to [Ho-Oh](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ho-Oh_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ho-Oh (Pokémon)")
     and cures it from bad statuses. This weather effect is only available in Rescue Team DX and is exclusive to the Ho-Oh boss fight at the peak of [Mt. Faraway](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mt._Faraway "Mt. Faraway")
    .
* **Riptide** (Japanese: **すいよせ**): Pokémon caught in a current will be swept away. Unidentified floating objects which appear as green sparkles will also be swept in. This weather effect is only available in Rescue Team DX and is exclusive to the [Lugia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lugia_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Lugia (Pokémon)")
     boss fight in [Silver Trench](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Silver_Trench "Silver Trench")
    .

In [Gates to Infinity](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
, Pokémon will regenerate HP only if the weather is Clear, or if it is holding a [Weather Band](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Scarf_(Mystery_Dungeon)#Weather_Band "Scarf (Mystery Dungeon)")
. In [Super Mystery Dungeon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
 and [Rescue Team DX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
, Pokémon can regenerate HP in any weather as long as they do not take damage from the weather condition, or if it is holding a [Weather Looplet](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Looplet#Weather_Looplet "Looplet")
 or Weather Band.

Moves and Wonder Orbs that activate weather cause it to last 18 turns**[RB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team")
**, indefinitely **[TD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness")
[S](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky")
****[GtI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity")
**, or 66 turns**[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
**, and override weather from Abilities. Weather Abilities activate at the start of a floor, lasting indefinitely and reactivating every 36 turns if overwritten**[RB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Red_Rescue_Team_and_Blue_Rescue_Team "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team")
****[TD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness")
[S](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky")
**, or last a random multiple of 66 turns and likewise reactivate a random multiple of 66 turns after expiring**[SMD](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon "Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon")
****[RTDX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
**, while [Mega Evolution](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mega_Evolution "Mega Evolution")
 Abilities persist so long as they are in effect. Certain dungeons also have a set or random weather effect on specific floors, as seen in the weather lists below. These last indefinitely, but can be overwritten.

The Abilities [Cloud Nine](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cloud_Nine_(Ability) "Cloud Nine (Ability)")
 and [Air Lock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Air_Lock_(Ability) "Air Lock (Ability)")
 cause the floor to have ineffective weather as long as there is at least one Pokémon on the floor with either of the aforementioned Abilities. Prior to Gates to Infinity, the Abilities change the weather on the floor to Clear and render Wonder Orbs, moves, and other Abilities that change the weather useless.

#### Cataclysm

The **cataclysms** are unique, one-of-a-kind weather conditions that are exclusive to battles with the [Tower duo](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tower_duo "Tower duo")
 and [super-ancient Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Super-ancient_Pok%C3%A9mon "Super-ancient Pokémon")
 within certain dungeons in [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Rescue_Team_DX "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX")
. These weather conditions have a very unique effect of moving Pokémon around, making the fights more challenging. The cataclysms last until the boss is defeated and can co-exist with other weather, with only Flooded and Rainbow Light categorized as [dungeon statuses](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dungeon_status "Dungeon status")
. Weather-based moves and abilities are unaffected by cataclysms.
