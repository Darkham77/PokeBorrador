#### Background

As seen in an ancient mural, thousands of years prior to the events of the manga, [Primal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') Kyogre battled Primal Ground in order to expand the oceans in [Hoenn](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn 'Hoenn') . Eventually though, Rayquaza appeared and defeated the two.

At some point later, Kyogre and Groudon awakened and battled once again. Since the two [Legendary Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Legendary Pokémon') were at a stalemate, they both traveled deep under the [Seafloor Cavern](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seafloor_Cavern 'Seafloor Cavern') to sleep.

#### [_Ruby & Sapphire_ arc](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ruby_%26_Sapphire_arc_(Adventures)> 'Ruby & Sapphire arc (Adventures)')

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/5/5e/Archie_and_Kyogre_Adventures.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Archie_and_Kyogre_Adventures.png)

Kyogre and Archie

[Team Aqua](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Aqua 'Team Aqua') sought to awaken Kyogre in order to fulfill their ultimate goal to expand the sea. Kyogre was first seen in _[Assaulted by Pelipper I](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS214 'PS214') _, sleeping at the bottom of the sea. In _[You Can Fight Day or Night With Lunatone & Solrock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS229 'PS229') _, Kyogre's [Blue Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blue_Orb 'Blue Orb') is stolen by [Blaise](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blaise 'Blaise') and later given to [Maxie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Maxie 'Maxie') .

In _[Walrein and Camerupt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS230 'PS230') _, after clashing with one another multiple times, Team Aqua and [Team Magma](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Magma 'Team Magma') formed a temporary alliance and worked together to reach the Seafloor Cavern, where Kyogre and Groudon rested. Upon [arriving](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS233 'PS233') at their destination via the [Submarine Explorer 1](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Submarine_Explorer_1 'Submarine Explorer 1') , both teams ended their truce and went their separate ways to find the chamber where their respective super-ancient Pokémon rested. In _[The Beginning of the End with Kyogre & Groudon II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS234 'PS234') _, Team Aqua managed to find Kyogre first and awaken it before Team Magma did the same with Groudon. Kyogre surfaces around [Route 108](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_108 'Hoenn Route 108') , where it began spreading panic and devastation.

[Later](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS242 'PS242') , to [Archie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Archie 'Archie') 's confusion, Kyogre stops its advance while Groudon continues causing havoc in the area where it surfaced. Believing the cause to be Team Magma, Archie has [Amber](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Amber_(Adventures)> 'Amber (Adventures)') go out to investigate. Amber discovers that Team Magma is in possession of two [orbs](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colored_orbs 'Colored orbs') that are capable of controlling Kyogre and Groudon. Amber finds Kyogre's orb, the Blue Orb, in the possession of [Tabitha](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tabitha 'Tabitha') and battles him in order to obtain it. After he succeeds, Amber gives the Blue Orb to Archie, who uses it to take control of Kyogre.

[After](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS244 'PS244') the [Pokémon Association](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Association 'Pokémon Association') is informed of Kyogre and Groudon's awakening, the [Gym Leaders](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gym_Leader 'Gym Leader') of Hoenn are dispatched to try and fight the super-ancient Pokémon. [Flannery](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Flannery 'Flannery') , [Wattson](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wattson 'Wattson') , and [Winona](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Winona 'Winona') are paired up against Kyogre, but are unable to do much against its defenses despite having a type-advantage. They are further impaired by Team Aqua's [Subleaders of the Sea Scheme](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aqua_Admin_(Trainer_class)#In_the_manga> 'Aqua Admin (Trainer class)') , who each fight the Gym Leaders one-on-one in order to let Kyogre advance without any further obstacles. Only one of the Subleaders manage to defeat the Gym Leaders, but they managed to stall long enough for Kyogre to escape.

In _[The Beginning of the End with Kyogre & Groudon X](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS248 'PS248') _, the prolonged usage of the orbs caused Archie and Maxie to be overwhelmed by the power of Kyogre and Groudon, causing them to be the ones possessed instead. [Ruby](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ruby_(Adventures)> 'Ruby (Adventures)') and [Sapphire](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sapphire_(Adventures)> 'Sapphire (Adventures)') attempt to battle the two leaders and retrieve the orbs. Though they manage to defeat Archie and Maxie, Ruby is unable to remove the orbs before they begin receding into the leaders' bodies. [With](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS249 'PS249') Kyogre and Groudon's power now surging inside of them, Archie and Maxie take every single person inside the Seafloor Cavern back to the surface and proceed to fly towards [Sootopolis City](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sootopolis_City 'Sootopolis City') , where Kyogre and Groudon plan to continue their age-old clash.

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/a/a4/Kyogre_Drizzle_Adventures.png/400px-Kyogre_Drizzle_Adventures.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Kyogre_Drizzle_Adventures.png)

[Drizzle](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Drizzle_(Ability)> 'Drizzle (Ability)') activated

[Upon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS250 'PS250') arriving at their destination, Archie and Kyogre begin battling against Maxie and Groudon. The clash between the super-ancient Pokémon causes destructive energy to begin spreading out. In _[The Beginning of the End with Kyogre & Groudon XIV](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS252 'PS252') _, with the power of the [Grand Meteor](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Meteorite 'Meteorite') and their Pokémon, Ruby and Sapphire force the orbs out of Archie and Maxie's bodies. The act causes a giant explosion to engulf the entirety of Sootopolis City and send Ruby and Sapphire flying. The two [Pokédex holders](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9dex_holder 'Pokédex holder') end up on [Mirage Island](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mirage_Island_(Generation_III)> 'Mirage Island (Generation III)') , where they learn that the [Red](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Red_Orb 'Red Orb') and Blue Orbs have been transferred into their bodies. As they stay on the island, Ruby and Sapphire train with [Juan](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Juan 'Juan') in order to learn how to use the Orbs without losing control of themselves. While they train, the [Hoenn Elite Four](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Elite_Four 'Hoenn Elite Four') , [Wallace](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wallace 'Wallace') , and [Steven Stone](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Steven_Stone 'Steven Stone') use the power of [Regirock](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Regirock_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Regirock (Pokémon)') , [Regice](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Regice_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Regice (Pokémon)') , and [Registeel](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Registeel_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Registeel (Pokémon)') to keep the power exerted by Kyogre and Groudon's clash contained before it spreads throughout Hoenn.

[After](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS259 'PS259') finishing their training, Ruby and Sapphire leave Mirage Island and return to Sootopolis. Due to worrying that Sapphire may suffer the same fate as Archie and Maxie, Ruby locks Sapphire in Wallace's aircar and hands the Blue Orb to [Courtney](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Courtney 'Courtney') . Ruby and Courtney use the Orbs to control Kyogre and Groudon, but due to a lack of training and interference from elsewhere, Courtney is eventually killed by the falling rubble of the [Cave of Origin](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cave_of_Origin 'Cave of Origin') . [Before](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS262 'PS262') succumbing to her wounds, Courtney tells Ruby that his father, [Norman](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Norman_(Adventures)> 'Norman (Adventures)') , is coming with [Rayquaza](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rayquaza_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Rayquaza (Pokémon)') to stop the fighting.

In _[It All Ends Now IV](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS263 'PS263') _, after Norman arrives with Rayquaza, Ruby takes both orbs and joins his father on top of the Sky High Pokémon. With Rayquaza's roar and the Blue and Red Orbs, Ruby and Norman defeat Kyogre and Groudon. With the battle over, Kyogre heads back to the Seafloor Cavern to continue its slumber.

[PS612](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS612 'PS612') , Archie takes Kyogre to the [Meteor Village](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Meteor_Village 'Meteor Village') . There, Kyogre is trained to learn [Origin Pulse](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Origin_Pulse_(move)> 'Origin Pulse (move)') in order to destroy the [meteoroid](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Grand_Meteor_Delta 'Grand Meteor Delta') threatening to destroy the world. After a day, Kyogre and Archie finish their training and exit from their training space. Just as they do so, Rayquaza sees Kyogre and Groudon through Hoopa's ring and attempts to attack them. As Rayquaza flies towards the ring, Hoopa gets scared, causing it to accidentally send Rayquaza to another ring connecting the village to [Route 120](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_120 'Hoenn Route 120') . Archie and Kyogre chase after Rayquaza by forcing their way through Hoopa's ring.

[Upon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS613 'PS613') arriving at Route 120, Archie and Maxie decide to test the moves Kyogre and Groudon learned on Rayquaza, but are interrupted by a large chunk of the meteoroid falling down on them. To destroy the chunk, Archie, Maxie, Ruby, and Sapphire combine the powers of Primal Kyogre, Primal Groudon, and Rayquaza into a single attack. Though the attack destroys the meteoroid chunk, pieces of the meteoroid rain down onto Kyogre and Groudon, knocking them out and reverting them into their base forms.

### Personality and characteristics

Kyogre is a powerful Pokémon, able to cause large scale tsunamis and tidal waves with little effort. It is very powerful in battle, easily shrugging off attacks from [Gym Leaders](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gym_Leader 'Gym Leader') . It strives for the expansion of the sea in the [Hoenn](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn 'Hoenn') region and has a long rivalry with [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') because of it. The Sea Basin Pokémon gets easily angered when its goal is interrupted. Kyogre has a large amount of stamina, able to battle for almost a month straight.

Groudon and Maxie

[Team Magma](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Magma 'Team Magma') sought to awaken Groudon in order to fulfill their ultimate goal to expand the land. #### Background

As seen in an ancient mural, thousands of years prior to the events of the manga, Rayquaza appeared to stop the fight between [Primal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') Kyogre and Primal Groudon.

Five years prior to the events of the manga, Rayquaza was sought after by the [Pokémon Association](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Association 'Pokémon Association') . The [Devon Corporation](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Devon_Corporation 'Devon Corporation') found it at the [Embedded Tower](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Embedded_Tower 'Embedded Tower') , however, the capture operation also led to the death of the [Draconid](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Draconid 'Draconid') Lorekeeper, Aster, who fought to protect Rayquaza. The Pokémon Association then researched Rayquaza in hopes of controlling it and prevent any disaster that may come if [Kyogre](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Kyogre (Pokémon)') and [Groudon](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Groudon_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Groudon (Pokémon)') were awakened.

As it did not possess a corresponding [orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colored_orbs 'Colored orbs') like its two counterparts, an artificial [Green Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Jade_Orb 'Jade Orb') was created in order to control Rayquaza. Before the orb could be finished, a [Salamence](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Zinnia%27s_Salamence "Zinnia's Salamence") came crashing into the research facility, destroying everything, injuring the scientists, and freeing Rayquaza back into the wild. When [Norman](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Norman_(Adventures)> 'Norman (Adventures)') found the [Salamence](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Salamence_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Salamence (Pokémon)') , he realized that it was injured in a previous battle with [Ruby](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ruby_(Adventures)> 'Ruby (Adventures)') . Fearing the Association's wrath towards his son, Norman kept this information hidden to protect him. As punishment for keeping secrets, the Association barred Norman from taking the test to become a [Gym Leader](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gym_Leader 'Gym Leader') for five years and forced him to find where Rayquaza fled to. Five years later, Norman managed to track Rayquaza to the [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') , where the Sky High Pokémon rested.

When it notices Kyogre and Groudon through the ring, Rayquaza goes wild and begins attacking. The attack scares Hoopa into accidentally moving the ring connecting Goldenrod City to the Meteor Village to another ring. Rayquaza flies through the ring and arrives in [Route 120](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hoenn_Route_120 'Hoenn Route 120') , where it proceeds to throw Ruby away. Ruby is saved from falling by Wally, who offers to subdue Rayquaza by battling against it. Ruby thanks Wally for his assistance but tells him to stop attacking. Ruby attempts to reason with Rayquaza and asks for both of them to team up in order to stop the meteoroid. Rayquaza accepts Ruby's offer and allows him to ride on its back.

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/b/be/Rayquaza_rejecting_Zinnia_Adventures.png/400px-Rayquaza_rejecting_Zinnia_Adventures.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Rayquaza_rejecting_Zinnia_Adventures.png)

Rayquaza stabbing Zinnia and rejecting her request

[After](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS613 'PS613') Ruby convinces [Sapphire](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sapphire_(Adventures)> 'Sapphire (Adventures)') to join him in riding Rayquaza, [Archie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Archie 'Archie') and [Maxie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Maxie 'Maxie') arrive with Kyogre and Groudon after having forced their way through Hoopa's ring. Archie and Maxie decide to test the moves Kyogre and Groudon learned on Rayquaza, but are interrupted by a large chunk of the meteoroid falling down on them. To destroy the chunk, Ruby, Sapphire, Archie, and Maxie combine the powers of Rayquaza, [Primal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Primal_Reversion 'Primal Reversion') Kyogre, and Primal Groudon into a single attack. Though the attack destroys the meteoroid chunk, pieces of the meteoroid rain down onto Kyogre and Groudon, knocking them out and reverting them into their base forms.

Zinnia arrives shortly [after](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS615 'PS615') only to find Ruby and Sapphire riding on top of Rayquaza. Angered that they managed to get Rayquaza, Zinnia, now wearing two Mega Anklets, challenges the two to a battle. Sensing the lingering presence of [Aster](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aster 'Aster') on the [Noivern](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Noivern_(Pok%C3%A9mon)> 'Noivern (Pokémon)') Zinnia used in the battle, Rayquaza is lured away from Ruby and Sapphire. After moving to another location, Zinnia reveals to Rayquaza about her attempts to free it from the Pokémon Association's control and asks it to join her in destroying the meteoroid. Angered, Rayquaza rejects Zinnia's request, impales her with its tail, and blasts her away into the sea. It then begins circling around Sootopolis City in a rage.

In _[Omega Alpha Adventure 21](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PAORAS21 'PAORAS21') _, Ruby and Sapphire are outfitted with the [Magma](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magma_Suit 'Magma Suit') and [Aqua Suits](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aqua_Suit 'Aqua Suit') needed to survive the trip to space. Once they are ready, Rayquaza swoops down and grabs the two in order to take them up into space. Upon coming face to face with Grand Meteor Delta, Ruby and Sapphire Mega Evolve Rayquaza and command it use Dragon Ascent. The attack, along with the combined forces of allies from Hoenn, [Kanto](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto 'Kanto') , and [Johto](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Johto 'Johto') , successfully destroys Grand Meteor Delta by shattering it into pieces. However, out from the meteoroid's wreckage emerges a Delta Shield, which opens to reveal a [Deoxys](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Deoxys_(Adventures)#Organism_No._1> 'Deoxys (Adventures)') , that proceeds to attack the three. After a tough battle, Ruby and Sapphire have Rayquaza strike Deoxys's core, shattering it and knocking Deoxys unconscious, causing it to begin floating to the depths of space. Afterward, Rayquaza grabs Ruby and Sapphire and takes them back down to Earth to reunite with their friends.

Afterward, Rayquaza leaves Ruby and Sapphire, but not before Ruby gives it [spicy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Flavor 'Flavor') [Pokéblocks](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9block 'Pokéblock') as a gift. As he reminisces about his time with Rayquaza, Ruby hopes that it will continue to protect the world in the future.

### Personality and characteristics

Rayquaza is an ancient [Legendary Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_Pok%C3%A9mon 'Legendary Pokémon') revered by the [Draconid people](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Draconid_people 'Draconid people') , who refer to it as the "Dragon Lord" (Japanese: **竜神様** _Dragon God_). According to the [Elder](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Elder_(Meteor_Village)> 'Elder (Meteor Village)') of the [Meteor Village](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Meteor_Village 'Meteor Village') , [Aster](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Aster 'Aster') , the previous Lorekeeper, was the only Draconid to ride on top of Rayquaza before her death. Rayquaza enjoys being around poisonous ozone layers, which it hibernates in. It also enjoys resting in ancient towers built by humans such as the [Embedded Tower](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Embedded_Tower 'Embedded Tower') and the [Sky Pillar](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sky_Pillar 'Sky Pillar') .

Unlike Kyogre and Groudon, Rayquaza has no orb to be controlled, so the [Pokémon Association](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Association 'Pokémon Association') attempted to create an artificial "[Green Orb](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Green_Orb 'Green Orb') ", however, it failed when Rayquaza escaped. Even though Rayquaza is worshipped as a god by Hoenn's people, including the Draconids, Rayquaza can also become really violent and aggressive to humans as seen when [Norman](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Norman_(Adventures)> 'Norman (Adventures)') controlled it and when Zinnia requested it to help destroy the [Grand Meteor Delta](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Grand_Meteor_Delta 'Grand Meteor Delta') in place of [Ruby](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ruby_(Adventures)> 'Ruby (Adventures)') and [Sapphire](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sapphire_(Adventures)> 'Sapphire (Adventures)') .
